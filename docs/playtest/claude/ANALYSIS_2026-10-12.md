## 1. What the evidence says

**Combat is not too hard; it is unreadable.** With the best available plan a turn costs ~0.8 HP (replay
audit; matches the designer). A Perfect is reachable on 93–100 % of turns. Screen testers land one on ~20 %,
exactly what taking no action gives, and deal ~4.5 per turn against ~18 for the best plan. Vision models
asked "would your pointer land on a Perfect if you sent now?" score 87 % where always answering "no" scores
90 %: they name the right slice but cannot see centre vs ±1 tick, with or without a full-resolution close-up.
Decision parity ranks the missing information: the predicted landing and its precision (20 of 20 probed
turns), the planned outcome on the HP chips (13), the pointer's offset (6), full card text (6).

**The campaign is survivable; the final boss is a wall.** The LLM strategist kept the home server at 50/50,
Heat at 40–48 and lost 0–4 operatives per campaign; all 3 Exploits by run 2–6. Then The Genome Core killed
11 of 12 breach attempts (operatives at Rank 22–35). Wins came at runs 30 and 52 (GDD 11.8: fast 8, average
24). The built-in policy (no strategy) never finishes: Heat 100, ~18 deaths. 1,300–1,900 Schematics end
unspent. Caveat: the planner looks one turn ahead; a multi-phase boss may punish that more than a person.

**The opening and the city cost the most time.** Finding a reachable Site cost ~2.5 h across 5 testers;
combat symbols (chips, negative RAM, the "1/0" Null, OVFL/SHIM/DFRG, "D 0") cost every combat tester.

## 2. Proposed change sets (for CP-003; nothing is planned before the rulings)

### A. Combat legibility (highest evidence; the cause of most deaths)
- **A1** Show the precision of the current setup before SEND IT: PERFECT / GOOD / WEAK on the pointer and in
  the preview, updating as cards and nudges are queued. Not tick marks (CP-002 ruling 7). (quiz, parity #1/#4, audit)
- **A2** Verify, then complete, the planned-outcome preview: chips show the queued plan's result incl. kills
  ("→ 0", ruling 5), chained across several queued actions. The live preview exists after aiming; the
  parity reviewer saw only turn starts, so check what is already there first. (parity #2)
- **A3** Label the combat chips and symbols in place: HP / RAM chips, negative "(-N)" RAM, the Null "1/0",
  OVFL / SHIM / DFRG, the "D 0" badge; a legend reachable from combat. (PB-010 C1, PB-012: 6 of 8 and 3 of 3)
- **A4** Full card text on the face (no "Blocked while th…"), with direction and tick distance. (C2, parity #5)
- **A5** Teach nudge / Free Nudge / Q-E in the tutorial; give Q/E a visible state. (C5)

### B. Flow bugs (confirmed by 2+ testers or clean steps)

Firmware that does not fit, toast ~0.25 s (4) · B3 an aim that Esc / Z / undo cannot clear (3) · B6 undo

## 3. Proposed to ignore (or hold)
- Input-related "click did nothing" items (PB-010 B4, B13 Enter, B14): posted input is not a real mouse;
  the confirm-dialog case was the tooling (designer checked). Hold until a human repeats them.
- Difficulty complaints and deaths from screen testers: skill-limited (section 1). No combat tuning.
- "Campaign too long" from screen testers: the time went to the Site hunt and the boss wall, not length.
- Weak-pick calls driven by unreadable or unusable cards (D4/A4 instead).
- Screen-audit polish rows (contrast nits, sticker overlaps on decorative art) unless a tester tripped.
- R7 as a list (CP-002 ruling 2): these findings replace it as the basis of the next plan.

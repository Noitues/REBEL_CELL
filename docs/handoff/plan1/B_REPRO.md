## Notes

- Harness limit found: headless Godot (GUT) drops pushed mouse events (`Input.parse_input_event` and `Viewport.push_input` both),
  even for a plain Button. Key events work. The mouse-dependent verdicts (B5, B7) are therefore logic-level; B7's
  hover-then-click sequence is the real order of events when a person points at the button, so it is a strong repro.
- The windowed attempt (`tools/run_windowed.py`, quiet window) does not give a usable mouse position; not repeated.
- Screens read for evidence: PB-010 quietcarrier 0319/0320, gridlock 0568/0587/0698/0701, neon_moth 0700, rustbelt 0026.

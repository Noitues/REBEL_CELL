# N1: Amount modes (FLAT / PERCENT; flat enemy amounts scale by the tier damage factor)

- **Goal**
  - Add `amount_mode` (FLAT / PERCENT) to heal, block, shield and corruption effects (R5), as a mode on the existing effects, not a new status.
  - Flat amounts on enemies are multiplied by the enemy's tier damage factor (R2). This fixes the hub HEAL, which ignores `output_scale`.
  - PERCENT reads the owner's max HP (corruption: share of the target's non-NULL slices, min 1) and is never scaled again.
  - Player FLAT amounts stay unscaled. Buffs and debuffs stay tier-independent.
- **Base / tag**: `<BASE_SHA>` / `<TEMP_TAG>`
- **Agent type and lane**: fix-rules. No Opus (the change is arithmetic with no new RNG or checkpoint use).
- **Depends on**: none. N2 later supplies the real `enemy_damage_by_tier` table. N1 reads a factor through `enemy_output_scale()` and is tested with a stub [1.0, 1.3, 1.69, 2.2].

## Files (owned)
- `scripts/data/effect_data.gd`, `scripts/data/slice_data.gd`: `amount_mode`, plus a PERCENT range check in `validate()`.
- `scripts/data/rc.gd`: `enum AmountMode { FLAT, PERCENT }` (append-only; FLAT = 0 so old `.tres` load FLAT).
- `scripts/core/effect_interpreter.gd`: `_scaled`, `heal`, `gain_block`, `gain_shield`, and the corruption count path.
- `scripts/core/combat_resolver.gd`: `_resolve_pointer` / `_slice_action` carry the mode, and the preview path shares the same call.
- Tests (new files only).

## Acceptance
1. An old `.tres` without the field loads as FLAT and resolves exactly as before. A golden replay of the existing seeds is unchanged for player effects.
2. Enemy FLAT = round(base × factor). Base 10 gives 10 / 13 / 17 / 22 at T1–T4. Hub HEAL now scales (previously ignored).
3. PERCENT = round-half-up(owner max HP × pct / 100), minimum 1 when pct > 0, 0 when pct = 0. Max 300 at 2 % gives 6 at every tier (not rescaled).
4. Player FLAT block at T4 stays at base.
5. Player shield stays capped at `shield_cap` = 15. Max 200 at 10 % gives 15. No enemy cap (Q9 is open).
6. Heal clamps to max HP and reports the real delta.
7. Corruption PERCENT is a share of non-NULL slices. 5 slices at 40 % gives 2, 1 slice at 10 % gives 1, 0 slices gives 0. FLAT N is capped by the available slices.
8. Preview equals result for every mode, on the resolver path and for the hub heal.
9. `validate()` rejects PERCENT above 100 or below 0 with a reason string.
10. No new randomness. The slice pick still uses the existing stream, and replay is identical.

- **Goal:** Rename the three defenses for display only: DEFRAG→BLOCK, SANDBOX-type→SHIELD, DETOUR→SANDBOX (R13). SANDBOX now negates one whole resolution (every hit of one slice resolution). Pierce ignores BLOCK and SHIELD, not SANDBOX or bodyguards. Enum ints and saves are unchanged.

## Acceptance
1. Display names read BLOCK, SHIELD and SANDBOX everywhere (HUD, Codex, card text, tooltips). `SliceType` and `EffectType` enum ints are unchanged. `test_codex_terms` and `test_us_english` stay green.
2. The check order in `deal_hit` is SANDBOX, then bodyguard/satellite, then BLOCK, then SHIELD, then HP. Example: block 4, shield 4, hit 6 → block 0, shield 2, HP unchanged.
3. SANDBOX negates all hits of one resolution. A 4-hit slice of 2 damage each deals 0, and SANDBOX is consumed once. Two resolutions at two pointers: the first is negated, the second lands. Order is by pointer index, then slice id, then node id.
4. Pierce still ignores BLOCK and SHIELD, but a SANDBOXed resolution deals 0 even when piercing. Bodyguards still absorb piercing hits.
5. SANDBOX does not stack: two gains still negate only one resolution. It expires at the owner's next turn start. BLOCK expires at the owner's own turn start only (not the opponent's). SHIELD persists and is capped at 15 (14+1 → 15, 15+1 → 15).
6. The preview chips equal the real result for SANDBOX, BLOCK and SHIELD cases, including staged actions and undo/rewind. A seeded replay hash is identical before and after the change on fixtures that contain no SANDBOX-negated multi-hit.
7. The sweep resolves every shipped class and enemy wheel at all 30 ticks with no error and with preview = result.
8. Old saves load unchanged (no version bump).

# N21: Resistance on every fight, refreshed each turn (designer ruling 2026-10-18, Q16 + Q31)

- **Goal:** every enemy has per-turn spin resistance inside its class range: **normal 1-2, elite and mini-boss 3-4,
  boss 5-6** (ranges in `campaign_config.tres`). Resistance is **zeroed at end of turn and regained at start of turn**,
  marked by a grease-pen **"+N"** on the enemy's resistance readout when it refreshes. Intent: each turn the player can
  afford to manipulate either their own wheel or the enemy's, not both; bypass / strip effects become valuable.
- **Base / tag:** `<BASE_SHA>` / `<TEMP_TAG>` (after N2 and N4 merge: shares `campaign_config`, `start_turn`).
- **Agent type and lane:** fix-rules. OPUS-BECAUSE: start/end-of-turn order, preview = result, every enemy re-authored.
- **Depends on:** N2 (config tables), N4 (`resistance_step` on EnrageData adds to the per-turn value).

## Files
- `scripts/data/campaign_config_data.gd` + `content/config/campaign_config.tres`: `resistance_by_class` (min/max per
  class). Schema change: log it in DECISIONS, add its check to `tools/schema_smoke_checks.gd`.
- `scripts/data/enemy_data.gd` (`validate`): the enemy's per-turn total (passive + hub at fight start, before ICE /
  Heat modifiers) must lie inside its class range; named reason with the content id.
- `scripts/core/combat_resolver.gd`: `start_turn` (gain) and the end-of-turn step (zero). Today `start_turn` restores
  `full_resistance() + resistance_carry`; decide how `resistance_carry` (effect_interpreter ~488) fits "zeroed at end
  of turn" without changing what the carry effect promises, and say so in `notes`.
- `content/enemies/*.tres`: migrate every enemy into its range (passive and hub values). Write
  `docs/handoff/N21_RESISTANCE.md`: one row per enemy, old → new, and why (keep hub flavor where the hub is the source).
- HUD: the "+N" mark (grease-pen style per `docs/STYLE_GUIDE.md`, static, reduce-effects safe) on refresh; strings via
  TextDb, US English, text 1.0. Find the HUD file with `context_slice.py --deps`.

## Acceptance
1. Config holds the four ranges; no number in code. `validate` rejects a normal at 3 and a boss at 4 by name.
2. Every shipped enemy validates (sweep test). ICE / Heat "+1 resistance" still stacks on top and may exceed the range.
3. End of turn → resistance 0; start of turn → full value (+ enrage `resistance_step` × step). A nudge on the enemy
   wheel after the refresh is absorbed per GDD 2.5; Flip / Respin stay blocked while resistance > 0.
4. Ghost's "first nudge ignores resistance", Strip, Jam and Hub Breach behave as before against the new values.
5. Preview = result across the refresh; undo never crosses the turn checkpoint; save round trip and seeded replay hash
   stable (no RNG read).
6. The "+N" mark shows the gained amount once per refresh, never on a fight with 0 gain.
7. Old saves load (resistance values come from content at fight start; no save version bump unless needed: report).

# N11: Pointers on any tick

- **Goal:** Replace the evenly spaced `EXTRA_POINTER_OFFSETS` with data-driven pointer ticks, so a wheel's pointers may sit on any tick 0-29 (GDD 2.1 amendment, R10). Phase pointer changes (MULTIPLY / MIGRATE / ORBIT) accept any tick. Close needles stay readable in the HUD. Preview and result share one path. Existing content must resolve identically without edits.
- **Base / tag:** `<BASE_SHA>` / `<TEMP_TAG>`
- **Agent type and lane:** fix-rules. No Opus: no RNG or save-format change beyond one optional field.
- **Depends on:** none. N12 owns slice geometry; `wheel_math` is not touched.

## Files
- `scripts/core/combat_resolver.gd`: remove `EXTRA_POINTER_OFFSETS`; pointer ticks come from data; `_pointer_ticks` is the single placement path for preview and resolve.
- `scripts/data/enemy_data.gd` and `scripts/data/boss_phase_data.gd`: optional `pointer_ticks` (Array[int]) and its `validate`. Both files already exist.
- `scripts/data/rc.gd` is read-only here.
- `scripts/ui/wheel/*` (needle drawing and chip layout; read the real filenames with `context_slice.py --deps`).
- `tests/test_manifest.json` and `content/config/campaign_config.tres` (the pointer cap value only).

## Acceptance
1. A pointer tick t under rotation r reads `(r + t) mod 30`. Ticks 1, 2, 3 and 29 resolve to slice 0 GOOD, slice 0 WEAK, slice 1 WEAK (offset -2), and slice 0 GOOD (offset -1). Over all 30 ticks there are exactly 6 PERFECT ticks and no miss tier.
2. Pointers (0, 14) at rotation +4 read ticks (4, 18). Two Shim hits land once per pointer, each with its own slice and tier. Duplicate ticks both resolve with no dedup. Ruling for Q1: duplicates are valid.
3. Default MULTIPLY with no authored ticks yields 15 and 10 spacing, identical to the old constants. A golden test over every shipped enemy and boss compares the old and new tick sets: 0 diffs.
4. MULTIPLY accepts [0, 14], [0, 11] and [0, 13]. MIGRATE to tick 13 is telegraphed on turn N and applied on N+1. ORBIT 3 from tick 28 reads 1, and the trail preview ticks equal the actual ticks over 3 turns.
5. `validate` rejects a tick outside 0-29 with `pointer_tick_out_of_range`, and more than 4 pointers with `too_many_pointers` (cap in config, not code).
6. The preview and the resolve call the same function: a sweep of 200 seeded turns shows the previewed pointer ticks, slices and tiers equal the resolved ones. Rewind, undo and the replay hash are unchanged. N11 draws no RNG.
7. A save written before N11 loads, and its pointers equal the legacy offsets. A save round-trip keeps authored ticks.
8. UI: two pointers 1 tick apart both render, and their chips are at least 24 px apart at 1080p and 1280x720, text scale 1.0. Chips and needles stay tied by color or number.

## Designer rulings 2026-10-18 (override the text above; DECISIONS "decision sheet" Q27)
- **Minimum needle gap is 1**: adjacent needles are valid (and wanted on some enemies); two needles on the **same tick
  are invalid**. Acceptance 2 changes: duplicate ticks are rejected by `validate` (`pointer_ticks_duplicate`), and the
  resolver never sees them. The legal [0, 1] pair resolves both.
- Every needle resolves and **each shows on the preview**, also when adjacent (acceptance 8 covers gap 1).
- Orbit and orbit-trail display stay as written (the designer's follow-up may change them later).

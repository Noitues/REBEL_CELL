## Acceptance
1. Same seed gives byte-identical `city.json` across two runs (NA02_FIELDS §1).
2. Over 1,000 seeds, every corp has an HQ rect overlapping no other HQ or building (`hq_overlaps` empty).
3. Every Site has a building (`bid`); every border link between two territories has a street.
4. Zero global RNG calls, proven by a grep-style test.
5. One seed generates under the config budget (placeholder 2 s); the 1,000-seed sweep asserts it.
6. Bake cache key changes with the seed, stable otherwise.
7. New campaign stores `city_seed`; save/load round-trips it; a resumed campaign regenerates an identical city. Old saves rejected cleanly per D3.
8. NeonCity renders a generated city without errors; `layout_seed` exported as u64 decimal string.

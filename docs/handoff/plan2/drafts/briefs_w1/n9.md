## Acceptance
1. OUTPUT_DOWN cuts output 25 % per stack, additive. 1 stack: 8 → 6; 2 stacks: 8 → 4; 4 or more stacks: 0, never negative. Rounding is half up and fixed.
2. TIER_DOWN lowers the landing tier one step per stack (Perfect → Good → Weak, floored at Weak). Perfect hooks do not fire on a downgraded landing.
3. The Botnet Perfect Trojan plants OUTPUT_DOWN ×2. Output is identical to the old halving on 20 sampled values.
4. `corrupted_ram_cost_multiplier` = 2 in config. Cards, paid nudges and the free-nudge accounting cost 2× while the acting needle reads a CORRUPTED slice. A cost of 0 stays 0.
5. The enemy-wheel Q37 halving applies to manipulation costs only (rounded up, minimum 1 where the base cost is above 0).
6. The retired fields are gone and content validation rejects them: `corrupted_self_damage`, `corrupted_damage_per_major`, `corrupted_ram_drain`. `_corrupted_trigger` deals no damage and drains no RAM.
7. OVERCLOCKED still burns out into CORRUPTED. ENCRYPTED absorbs one new status instance.
8. Forecast = result. The `ram_bar` chip shows exactly the function's number. Undo and rewind refund the exact amount, and a seeded replay hash is unchanged.
9. Codex and tutorial text exist as TextDb keys in US English.

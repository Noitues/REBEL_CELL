## Acceptance
1. New Trigger entries appended; existing enum ints unchanged (a test pins them).
2. `ListenerData` holds trigger, effects, `max_per_turn`, `max_per_combat`, optional min tier. `validate()` rejects empty effects, cap ≤ 0, and RNG-dependent effects (RESPIN, random SlicePick).
3. Listeners fire only after an accepted action. Free nudge fires ON_NUDGE, not ON_RAM_SPENT (amount 0); paid nudge fires ON_RAM_SPENT with its amount.
4. ON_PERFECT fires once per Perfect needle (2 needles = 2 fires). ON_HIT_DEALT fires after block and shield are consumed, carrying final damage.
5. Listener damage uses tier factor (FLAT) or max HP (PERCENT, unscaled). It does not trigger listeners (recursion guard).
6. Per-turn counters reset in `start_turn`; per-combat counters persist and survive a mid-turn save round trip.
7. Preview equals result for each trigger, with no "?" parts. Compound plays show every known result.
8. Undo and rewind reverse listener effects and counters, never crossing a checkpoint. Seeded replay gives identical state hash, with no RNG stream draws.
9. Order: player action, then listeners by (enemy id, listener index); in resolve: defensive pass, offensive pass, then listeners.
10. One enemy HUD chip shows the preview number, no new motion.

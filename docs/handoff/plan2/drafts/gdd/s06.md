# gdd: section 6 amendment

## 1. Summary

Section 6 (Firmware, Daemons & Inner Rings) changes in five areas:
- **Firmware** gains tick-quality and satellite-movement chips. It also gets a rule that Firmware is anti-status: statuses are anti-Firmware.
- **Daemons** are re-keyed to the new rules: corruption as a RAM cost, the defense trio, enemy listeners, satellite replacement, and the shop and rewards hooks.
- **Inner Rings** keep their rules, with two touch-ups. Precision stays measured on the outer ring, and the ring's readability redesign is flagged as pending (G1).
- **Acquisition** is rewritten around Level I-IV tiers, the data-driven rewards list, Rack flash, Rest and Workshop nodes, the shop wheel and Cache.
- **Not touched:** the principle that Firmware changes what a slice does, the one-socket-per-slice rule, and the existing Firmware and Daemon effects that are not named below. Phases, enrage, boss HP, parking, amount modes and the Parasite ring are enemy-side rules for §2 and §4 and are only cross-referenced here. Raids, Sync Strike, corporations, Site names, the grid zoom, D3, D5-D10 and the text 1.0 scope do not touch this section, except where §6.3 names where Firmware and Daemons come from.

## 2. Amendments

**A1. Section 6 intro rule.** Replace "**Rule:** Firmware changes what its slice does. Inner Ring segments change wheel state or modify the outer slice. Daemons are run-wide rules. Rule-breaking Daemons are expected." with:

> **Rule:** Firmware changes what its slice does, and how its sub-ticks read. Inner Ring segments change wheel state or modify the outer slice. Daemons are run-wide rules. Rule-breaking Daemons are expected.
> **Statuses are anti-Firmware** (2.9): they change how a slice performs (output, landing tier, RAM cost), never what it does. Firmware is the player's permanent answer to that pressure; Cleanse and Encrypt are the temporary one.
> **Tiers.** Firmware, Daemons and slices are upgraded in Levels (Level I, II, III, IV…). Levels are named this way to avoid confusion with Site tiers (T1-T4). Level upgrades are a separate design pass; numbers below are Level I.
> **Satellites** (2.7) are fixed to their slice. Only Firmware or Daemons marked "moves satellites" (6.1, 6.2) and one operative class may move, nudge or respin them.

**A2. 6.1 Firmware table.** Keep all six rows, with these edits and additions.

Replace the Hardened row with:
> | Hardened | Slice permanently ENCRYPTED (absorbs the next status; it is restored at the start of each combat, not each turn) (placeholder) |

Replace the Burner row's "(1.5×)" clause with "(1.5×, 2.9)". Its Heat cost is unchanged.

Add after the table:

> **Tick-quality Firmware.** A slice's sub-ticks each read WEAK, GOOD or PERFECT by distance from the slice centre (2.4, amended). This Firmware changes that map:
> | Firmware (working name) | Effect |
> |---|---|
> | Calibrated | Every sub-tick of the slice reads one tier higher (WEAK→GOOD, GOOD→PERFECT). A 4-tick slice (W G G W) reads G P P G. |
> | Narrow Band | The slice is 2 ticks smaller (taken from its neighbours' sizes by loadout rule) and its Perfect tick pays ×1.5 more (placeholder) |
> | Wide Band | The slice is 1 tick larger and its WEAK ticks read GOOD (placeholder) |
>
> Slice sizes must still sum to 30. A Firmware that changes size is refused at the socket if the wheel cannot absorb it. The loadout screen shows the resulting sub-tick map of every slice (4.x loadout), and the shop shows the map before and after a purchase.
>
> **Satellite Firmware.**
> | Firmware (working name) | Effect |
> |---|---|
> | Tow Hook | Satellites docked on this slice (and neighbours' in the same turn) may be moved: your nudges and spins may carry a chosen satellite one slice (placeholder) |
>
> **Socket rules.** One socket per slice. Installing Firmware into an occupied socket requires a confirm that names the chip it destroys (shop, Rack and event offers alike).

**A3. 6.1 Mirror and Shunt rows.** Append a note after the table text:

> Mirror and Shunt read neighbours by slice order, not by tick count. With variable slice sizes, "landing clockwise of centre" means a sub-tick on the clockwise side of that slice's own centre tick; a slice with an even size has no centre tick, so neither side is "centre" and Mirror copies the side landed on (no Perfect case) unless the slice is raised by Calibrated.

**A4. 6.2 Daemons table.** Replace the following rows:

Replace the Twin Pointer row's effect with:
> Your wheel is also read at a second pointer 15 ticks away (any tick the loadout allows, placeholder); both trigger. Max RAM halved.

Replace the Linked Bus row with:
> Nudging an enemy wheel (nudge actions, not cards) also moves your wheel the same way, free. Enemy nudge locks (2.5) also lock this Daemon.

Replace the Botnet Seed row with:
> Each Perfect deploys a 1-HP drone on the triggered slice (max 2). A drone replaces any drone already docked there.

Replace the Fault Tolerance row's effect with:
> NULL slice deals 3 damage to the pointer target (flat; scales by Level, not by tier).

Add rows:

> | Daemon (working name) | Effect |
> |---|---|
> | Clean Slate | Corrupted slices cost 1.5× RAM instead of 2× (rounded up) (placeholder) |
> | Shop Key | The shop wheel offers one free nudge per visit |
> | Rack Tithe | Server Rack flash (6.3) offers one extra option |
> | Tow Rope | Satellites and drones may be moved with your nudges and spins |
> | Counter-Listener | Enemy listeners (2.12) trigger one fewer time per combat (placeholder) |
> | Phase Cleanse | Your statuses are cleansed when an enemy enters a new phase |
> | Sandbag | The first enemy attack each combat that would reach you is negated as SANDBOX (once per combat) |

Add after the table:

> **Daemon categories (for the rewards list).** Heat Daemons (Clean Signal, Cold Exit, Scrubber), combat Daemons, economy Daemons (Shop Key, Rack Tithe) and satellite Daemons (Tow Rope, Botnet Seed). The data-driven rewards list (6.3) may filter by category.

**A5. 6.2 Stolen Intent.** Append: "Swapping resolved slices does not carry statuses; each slice keeps its own statuses (2.9)."

**A6. 6.3 Acquisition.** Replace the whole subsection with:

> ### 6.3 Acquisition
> What can be offered, where and when is set by the **rewards list**, a data table, not by code. Each row gives a kind (Firmware, Daemon, card, slice, Cycles, Schematics, operative, asset, flash), a pool, a count, a chance and conditions (node type, elite, Rack, tier, layer). Numbers below are defaults (placeholder).
>
> | Source | Default offer |
> |---|---|
> | Router | Common Firmware or a card |
> | Elite Router | Uncommon Firmware or a Daemon |
> | Terminal (event) | Events may offer Firmware, Daemons or rescued operatives (5.4), and may alter future fights or paths |
> | Mainframe (shop) | Everything, for Cycles; the shop wheel (6.3.1) |
> | Rest | A choice of: heal, or swap two Firmware chips between slices (no chip is destroyed) |
> | Workshop | Raise one slice, card or Firmware one Level for Cycles, no random choice |
> | Cache | An event mini-puzzle with a Firmware, Daemon or Cycles payout; no combat unless an option says so (subject to vetting) |
> | Server Rack | Rare Daemons + Schematics + **flash** (6.3.2) |
>
> There is no Intel, Relay or Ambush node. Safehouse and Heat Sink are City Grid nodes, not netrun nodes (3.2). A Black Market is at most an unknown event outcome.
>
> **6.3.1 Shop wheel.** The shop's slice-upgrade wheel has three slices priced 100 / 80 / 100 Cycles (placeholder; 11.2 sets prices). A shop nudge may be bought (1 RAM-equivalent price 20 Cycles, placeholder); a Daemon (Shop Key) makes the first nudge each visit free. Slice removal ("recycle bin") and Firmware replacement both require confirms (6.1).
>
> **6.3.2 Rack flash.** Capturing a Server Rack also offers a flash: choose one (a) Level up one slice, (b) swap one slice for an offered slice, or (c) install an offered Firmware. Flash is a row in the rewards list, so its availability by tier, layer and Rack is tunable. Capturing a final Rack still banks its Exploit (4.2).
>
> **6.3.3 Levels.** A slice, card, Firmware or Daemon has Levels I-IV. Level I is the base. Each Level raises a number or unlocks a rider and costs Cycles at a Workshop or a flash. Level numbers are set per item in content. Slice and card upgrades are separate design passes.

**A7. 6.4 Inner Ring Segments.** Keep the segment list. Add at the end:

> Precision tiers are measured on the outer ring only, using each slice's own sub-tick map (2.4).
> The Inner Ring's readability and manipulation are pending a redesign (G1): its segments must be readable at a distance, and it must be clear how to move them. Until then the rules above stand. The Corrupt segment applies CORRUPTED (2.9): the target's slice then costs the target's owner double RAM; on an enemy slice it applies the enemy-side rule in 2.9. Pierce ignores BLOCK and SHIELD, not SANDBOX and not bodyguards.
> ×2 moving to Firmware is deferred with G1; the segment stays until then.

**A8. New 6.5 Status interaction and Firmware.** Insert:

> ### 6.5 Statuses, Firmware and Levels
> - OUTPUT_DOWN reduces a slice's output 25% per stack, before Firmware multipliers like Patch+ (additive multiplier then reduction, placeholder).
> - TIER_DOWN lowers each landing by one tier per stack. Calibrated is applied first, so a Calibrated slice under one TIER_DOWN reads as its normal map.
> - ENCRYPTED absorbs the next status; Hardened refreshes it at combat start.
> - Statuses survive enemy phase changes (2.11); a status moves to the new slice that covers the old slice's centre tick, and stacks merge. A Phase Cleanse Daemon or a Cleanse effect removes them.
> - Statuses survive being moved by Firmware or a nudge; Firmware stays with its slice.
> - CORRUPTED is a RAM debuff (2.9), not damage. OVERCLOCKED (including Burner) ends in CORRUPTED as before.

## 3. Contradictions with other sections

- **§2.1:** "6 slices of 5 ticks" and "evenly spaced pointers" conflict with variable slice sizes (sum 30) and pointers on any tick. Section 6.1's Calibrated and Mirror rows depend on the §2.4 sub-tick map.
- **§2.4:** the precision table (offset 0, ±1, ±2) must become the per-tick quality map (size 2: G G; 3: G P G; 4: W G G W; 5: W G P G W; 6: W W G G W W; 7: W W G P G W W).
- **§2.5:** needs "block nudge / block respin" locks; §6.2 Linked Bus references it. Corrupted slices cost 2× RAM.
- **§2.6:** DEFRAG→BLOCK, SANDBOX→SHIELD, DETOUR→SANDBOX renames; §6.4 uses the new trio words.
- **§2.7:** "can be nudged individually" and "Spinning or flipping the host carries docked satellites along" must become fixed satellites with REPLACE on docking. "Pierce ignores block and shield, not satellites" gains "not SANDBOX".
- **§2.9:** CORRUPTED self-damage text, OVERCLOCKED, ENCRYPTED; the new statuses OUTPUT_DOWN, TIER_DOWN, ×N stacks.
- **§2.11 and §8.4 and §8.4b-d:** phases, parking, enrage, buffs and Exploits; PARASITE as a status vs the Parasite ring; boss names.
- **§3.2:** Safehouse and Heat Sink as grid-view nodes; 7 corporations in §8.
- **§4.2:** node types table needs Rest, Workshop, Cache; Intel/Relay/Ambush stay out; Site names removed.
- **§5.2:** the Breaker class's Perfect hook and station bonus (G8); one class moves satellites; the Botnet hook mentions Parasite drones, which collide with the new Parasite ring term.
- **§11:** shop prices (11.2) 100 / 80 / 100; economy of flash and Workshop.
- **§2.12 (new)** is assumed to hold enemy listeners; if that is numbered otherwise, §6.2's Counter-Listener row must be renumbered.

## 4. Not covered / needs a ruling

- Exact Level I-IV values for Firmware and Daemons (separate pass).
- Which operative class moves satellites (the ruling says "one class"; Tow Hook and Tow Rope are placeholders until that is named).
- Whether the Inner Ring's ×2 moves to Firmware, pending the G1 redesign.
- How the free first nudge in the shop is priced in RAM-equivalents (shop nudges use Cycles in my draft).

# gdd: section 9 amendment

## 1. Summary
Section 9 (UX & Visual Direction) changes where the 2026-10-16 rulings add things the player must read or the visual layer must carry:
- Combat readability now covers phases, parked damage, enrage, the sub-tick quality map, uneven pointers, ×N stacks, the CORRUPTED RAM cost, the Parasite ring, fixed satellites, listeners, locks and the defense trio.
- Raids and the Grid gain the zoom between the Grid view and the netrun (D6), the Heat chip and "RAID AT n" tick (D9), the less-intrusive full graph with hover highlight (D7), the new node types, access keys and Exploit buildings (D5), and the raid modifiers R1–R4.
- Sites lose their names.
- The text 1.0 input scope is mouse and keyboard.

The visual baseline (9.1 media table, layer rules, DOWN bolt, "the city reacts", the screen-glitch option) is kept. Section 10 and the rest of the GDD are untouched, except as listed in section 3 of this document.

**A4. In 9.2, after the sentence ending "(DECISIONS 2026-10-05, ruling 10).", insert the following new rules, numbered 9.2.1–9.2.10:**

"**9.2.1 Phases and parked damage.** An enemy with phases shows a phase pip row (one pip per phase, the current one lit) and a threshold tick on its HP bar. While the HP floor is active, a hit that would cross the next threshold shows **PARKED −N** on the enemy's result chip (the excess is lost). The phase line and the new layout reveal on entry. Where a boss buff hides them (Chart Hidden), they are revealed only on entry.

**9.2.2 Enrage.** A turn-based enrage shows as a countdown chip beside the enemy HP bar ("ENRAGE IN n"), then as a step badge (×1.25, ×1.5, …) once active. Enrage raises damage only. The chip is a live value and never sits on a sticker.

**9.2.3 Boss buffs and Exploits.** A boss's active buffs are listed as small tags under its name. A buff the route's Exploits have turned off is struck through and names the Exploit that did it. A buff whose counter Exploit was not drawn for this Grid is shown plainly, with no hint of a counter.

**9.2.4 Sub-tick quality.** Every slice displays its tick quality map (WEAK / GOOD / PERFECT per tick) on the loadout screen, and on the combat wheel as the tick strip under the pointer. Slice widths on the wheel are drawn at their true tick size. Firmware that raises tick quality redraws the map. A slice of 4 or fewer ticks that has no Perfect tick says so on its map. Pointers sit on any tick; when two pointers are within 3 ticks of each other, their labels offset so both stay readable.

**9.2.5 Statuses.** Statuses are anti-Firmware (they change how a slice performs, never what it does). The overlay is still the status. A ×N stack tab or multiplier tag appears on the corner badge for stacked statuses (OUTPUT_DOWN ×N, TIER_DOWN ×N) and shows the effective output or tier reading. CORRUPTED is shown as a RAM-cost overlay: a card or nudge that acts where the acting pointer reads a CORRUPTED slice shows its doubled RAM cost in the RAM forecast before commit. Statuses are not wiped by a phase change; where a layout changes, the overlay moves with its slice.

**9.2.6 Parasite ring.** Parasites are anti-slices: they sit on an outer partial ring on the player's wheel and rotate with it. They are drawn with the red enemy marking and a glyph for what they do (damage, reduce your damage, apply or remove a status, drain RAM). When one is read by the pointer, its result appears on the same chips as a slice's. Cleanse removes one segment.

**9.2.7 Satellites.** A satellite is drawn fixed to its slice. Its wheel shows no nudge control unless the operative's class, a Firmware or a Daemon can move satellites. A satellite that replaces another plays a replace animation, and the old one leaves.

**9.2.8 Listeners and locks.** An enemy listener (a reaction to the player's nudge, card, Perfect, RAM spend or hit) is shown as a small trigger glyph on the enemy, and a staged action's preview includes the listener's reaction (it undoes with the action). A locked nudge or respin is greyed with the source and the turns remaining; a refused action says why.

**9.2.9 Defense trio.** Three defenses are always distinguished by name, glyph and chip: BLOCK (temporary, expires at the start of your next turn), SHIELD (lingers, capped at 15) and SANDBOX (negates one whole attack). A PIERCE attack is marked on the incoming chip: it skips BLOCK and SHIELD, never SANDBOX or a bodyguard.

**9.2.10 Rim gates.** Gates sit on the wheel rim. The preview lights every gate a staged nudge or spin would cross. The respin never crosses gates."

**A5. Replace the whole text of 9.3 with:**

"### 9.3 Raids & Grid
**Views.** The Grid view (the full City Grid, where the campaign is monitored and the next netrun is chosen) stays. The storyboard begins in the full Grid view; JACK IN takes you into the netrun by a zoom dive. During a netrun the player may zoom out to the Grid view and back in to the netrun at will, with the same transition. There is no separate GRID VIEW toggle page.

**Grid chips.** The map shows a Heat chip and a "RAID AT n" tick at the next raid threshold.

**Netrun map.** The whole graph is drawn at all times in a less intrusive look; hover and the map legend bring a node, or a whole type, to the full active state with all its information. There is no hiding of nodes beyond the next layer and no "show all nodes" option. Node types are drawn by their type sticker. The netrun node types are Router, Elite Router, Terminal (event), Mainframe (shop), Rest (heal, or swap Firmware locations), Workshop, Cache and Server Rack; Safehouse and Heat Sink are Grid-view nodes. There is no Intel, Relay or Ambush node.

**Access keys.** Each access key is an Exploit, and each Exploit has its own building on the Grid, shown at its Site.

**Raids.** Threat paths drawn on links in red grease pencil, the Cell's plans in yellow (solid = will happen, dashed = what-if); exact projected outcomes per node during setup; playout speed controls and skip; post-raid summary and a raid-win reward screen. Raid modifiers appear as follows: an EXPOSED node carries a spotlight marker; encrypted routes are drawn dashed until decrypted; threat vehicle types have distinct glyphs (flying, heavy, lander); a second wave shows an ultimatum with its Schematics or Heat cost before the raid starts.

**Territory states.** **DOWN** is shown the same way at every zoom, on the Grid and in the raid view: a white bolt over a greyed marker (3.3). A DOWN node is a source for no one; a TAKEN node is corporation territory. Neither can launch netruns. Nodes cut off behind a TAKEN or DOWN chokepoint stay powered and are not shown as closed.

**HQ netruns.** An HQ netrun may run longer than 7 layers and is a unique experience per corporation, with its own mechanics; its presentation follows its design pass."

"### 9.4b Run-Layer Screens
- **Events.** Event choices that alter a future fight or the path show the change as a tag on the choice (for example 'next fight: enemy frozen'). An event that starts an impromptu fight says so before the player commits. - **Shop.** The shop wheel sells its three slices at 100 / 80 / 100 (placeholder). A shop-wheel nudge is bought for Cycles; a Daemon gives the first one free. Replacing a socketed Firmware asks for confirmation and names the chip it destroys."

## 3. Contradictions with other sections
- **Header "Platform: PC (mouse + keyboard, gamepad)":** it conflicts with the text 1.0 scope in A8; the header needs aligning.
- **2.1:** "6 slices of 5 ticks", "evenly spaced pointers", "Satellites 10 or 15 ticks" contradict variable slice sizes and pointers on any tick.
- **2.4 / 2.6 / 2.7 / 2.9:** the precision tiers by offset, the defense names and types, satellites "nudged individually" and the status table (CORRUPTED damage) all conflict with the rulings.
- **2.11:** it is the boss pointer phases section; it needs the phase, parking and enrage rules.
- **2.2:** the "random respin" step must allow respin locks.
- **4.2:** "Router / Terminal / Mainframe" node list lacks Rest, Workshop and Cache, and the 7-layer fixed length conflicts with HQ runs.
- **4.1:** "30–40 Sites" and the fixed tier chains conflict with the new procedural city.
- **3.3:** the DOWN/TAKEN recovery text needs the G11 rules.
- **Section 8 / 11:** the corporation count (5 vs 7) and the Site names.
- **11.x:** the damage and HP scale values.
- **Section 5.2:** the station bonuses need to be unique per class.

### 13.1 Decided, not yet built
These rules are decided (designer rulings 2026-10-16). Sections 2–11 describe the target behavior. Implementation follows the build order in the content design document. Anywhere text in this GDD describes the older behavior, the ruling wins.

1. **Fight structure.**
   - Phases are the unit of fight length: normal 1, elite 2, mini-boss 2–3, HQ boss 4–5. Each phase lasts 3–4 turns and asks a new question; no phase is attrition.
   - Damage parks at a phase threshold: a hit that would cross it leaves HP at the threshold and the excess is lost.
   - Health triggers phases. A turn-based enrage raises enemy damage only. Defaults (placeholder): boss and mini-boss from turn 10, elite from turn 8, every 5 turns, +25 % per step; normals have no enrage.
   - Statuses survive phase changes. A phase may cleanse.
   - Every enemy, mini-boss and boss is designed directly; the shared boss template is retired.
2. **Tier and amounts.**
   - Tier sets base values only (starting HP and damage). HP is authored per tier (`hp_by_tier`) and, for bosses, per ICE zone (`hp_bands`).
   - Flat enemy amounts scale by the enemy's tier damage factor.
   - Percent amounts read the owner's max HP and are never scaled again.
   - Buffs and debuffs are tier-independent.
   - Heal, block, shield and corruption come in FLAT and PERCENT modes.
3. **Wheel geometry.**
   - Pointers may sit on any tick (amends 2.1).
   - Slices may have any size from 2 to 7 ticks, odd or even. Each sub-tick has its own quality, shown on the loadout screen (amends 2.1 and 2.4).
   - A Firmware idea raises every sub-tick of a slice one tier.
   - Rim gates are kept and scheduled before the Orbital mini-boss.
4. **Statuses, corruption and the Parasite ring.**
   - Statuses are anti-Firmware: they change how a slice performs. Statuses carry ×N stacks.
   - CORRUPTED is a RAM debuff: a card or nudge acting on a CORRUPTED slice costs double RAM, and CORRUPTED deals no damage.
   - Parasites are anti-slices on a Parasite ring: an enemy-owned partial ring on the player wheel that can damage the player, reduce the player's damage, apply or remove statuses and drain RAM.
5. **Satellites** are fixed to their slice and cannot be nudged, spun, respun or changed. Exceptions: one operative class and some Firmware or Daemons. A satellite may replace a satellite on an occupied slice.
6. **Enemy interaction.**
   - Enemy listeners react to the player's actions (nudges, cards, Perfects, RAM spent, hits dealt).
   - Block-nudge and block-respin effects exist.
   - Listeners and locks must keep the preview equal to the result.
7. **Player defense trio.** BLOCK (temporary), SHIELD (lingers, capped at 15) and SANDBOX (negates one whole attack).
8. **Boss buffs and Exploits.** Bosses carry many strong buffs, each turned off by a named Exploit. A Grid draws a subset of the corporation's Exploits, so not every buff is counterable each run. Each access key equals an Exploit, and each Exploit has its own building.
9. **Progression and economy.**
   - Slice and card upgrades are separate passes with Levels I, II, III, IV…
   - The rewards list is data-driven. A Server Rack may offer a "flash" (upgrade or swap a slice).
   - Each class has a unique station bonus.
   - The shop wheel prices its three slices at 100 / 80 / 100 (placeholder). A shop-wheel nudge is bought or granted by a Daemon, which makes the first one free.
10. **Campaign structure.**
   - 7 corporations. The city uses the new procedural layout; old campaign saves are dropped.
   - A REST node is added (heal, or swap Firmware locations), plus Workshop and Cache nodes (Cache pending vetting). Safehouse and Heat Sink are grid-view nodes. There is no Intel, Relay or Ambush node in normal runs.
   - The raid / City Grid view stays, with a zoom between the grid and the netrun.
   - The map shows a Heat chip and a "RAID AT n" tick.
   - Nodes cut off behind a TAKEN or DOWN chokepoint stay powered. DOWN is a source for no one; TAKEN is corporation territory. Neither can launch netruns.
   - HQ runs may exceed 7 layers, and each is unique.
11. **Events.** Events may alter future fights or paths, start impromptu fights, use an event wheel, and appear in the HQ view or a raid-win screen.

### 13.2 Scheduled design passes
Each pass is a separate design pass with its own rulings. Nothing below is decided beyond what is stated:
- **Raid design.** All four modifiers in one pass: R1 EXPOSED spotlight, R2 encrypted routes, R3 vehicle types, R4 Heat-scaled second wave with an ultimatum (the Schematics sink).
- **DISPATCH Sync Strike.** A small fight per operative, then the final fight. Open: whether the minis dictate effects in the final, and whether the final rotates focus across the three player wheels before End Turn.
- **Events.** More events first; consolidation and pruning later.
- **Slice upgrades and card upgrades,** with the Rack flash row.
- **Inner ring redesign.** It does not read from afar, and what it does and how to work it is unclear. It comes before the Parasite ring UI. Slice rearranging is not a priority, maybe never.
- **Route and HQ redesign,** including the unique per-corporation HQ netruns.
- **Enemy direct-design pass.** Order is worst first, starting with the Triage Unit.
- **Corporation content** for Aurelian Concord and Tethys Deep: enemies, elites, mini-boss, boss, story paths, HQ netrun.

### 13.3 Open rulings
Questions the rulings left open (placeholder answers in parentheses where a default is proposed):
1. Reference player for HP bands: bot, testers or midpoint.
2. Enrage steps: additive or compounding.
3. The effect of CORRUPTED on enemy wheels now that it is a RAM debuff.
4. Whether any 3 of the drawn Exploits open the breach, or the Intel / Breach / Virus gate types stay fixed; the number drawn per Grid (5).
5. Which operative class moves satellites, whether Undock stays, and whether a replaced satellite counts as destroyed.
6. Whether enemy percent shields have their own cap.
7. Whether the Parasite ring rotates with the player wheel (proposed: yes).
8. Whether statuses apply on a WEAK landing (proposed: yes).
9. Boss win-rate target at ICE 0 (baseline 25 % fresh, 8 % in campaigns).

### 13.4 Out of scope for text 1.0
Text 1.0 targets PC with mouse and keyboard. Gamepad support, listed in the header, is deferred and not part of 1.0. Card type bands are closed as is (no per-card field).

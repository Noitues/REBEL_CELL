# gdd: section 5 amendment

## 1. Summary
- Section 5 absorbs the defense trio names (BLOCK / SHIELD / SANDBOX), the sub-tick quality map and slice sizes on operative wheels, fixed satellites and Botnet drone rules, corruption as a RAM cost, and the Level I–IV slice upgrade tiers.
- Class table gains the renamed slice types and a unique station bonus per class (G8). Alternate-version bonuses are no longer allowed to feel like the base class.
- Rank gains two lines: Rack flash and a link to Level tiers. Tier access (T1–T4) is unchanged.
- Stationing (5.4) is retargeted: the Safehouse is a grid-view node, not a netrun node. Roster rules are otherwise unchanged.
- Not changed: HP, RAM economy, Hub Core passives, Perfect hooks, permadeath, rank = netruns survived, 2 rookies / 20 Schematics start.
- Rulings that don't touch Section 5: boss HP bands, threshold parking, enrage, enemy phases, amount modes on enemy effects, Parasite ring internals, enemy listeners, rim gates, Sync Strike, Aurelian / Tethys, node types, events, Exploit buildings, zoom, Heat chip, raid modifiers, Site names, G11, procedural city. Section 5 cross-references only the ones listed below.

**A3. Insert new subsection 5.2b "Slice sizes and tick quality" after the M6 paragraph:**

5.2b Slice sizes and tick quality
1. Operative wheel slices may be 2–7 ticks, even sizes included. A wheel's slice sizes sum to 30 and a wheel may hold more or fewer than 6 slices.
2. Landing quality is read per tick by distance *d* from the slice centre: d = 0 PERFECT, d ≤ 1 GOOD, otherwise WEAK. Even-sized slices have no single centre tick, so their default maps are:

| Size | Default tick map |
|---|---|
| 2 | G G |
| 3 | G P G |
| 4 | W G G W (no Perfect) |
| 5 | W G P G W (the starting slice) |
| 6 | W W G G W W |
| 7 | W W G P G W W |

3. A slot may author its own tick map. The loadout screen shows every slice's map.
4. A size-4 or size-6 slice cannot be Perfect, so Perfect hooks and Perfect-based effects never trigger from it unless Firmware changes the map.
5. Pointers may sit on any tick (amends 2.1), so quality depends on where a pointer stands relative to each slice.

**A4. In 5.2, replace the "RAM:" paragraph with:**

RAM: start each combat with 6, +4 per turn, carry-over, max 12. A card or nudge used where the acting pointer reads a CORRUPTED slice costs double RAM (2×, placeholder). CORRUPTED no longer damages the operative or drains RAM by itself (amends 2.9); damage that used to come from it now comes from the Parasite ring. All classes share a card pool; each class adds 1–2 exclusive cards.

**A5. In 5.2 "Botnet drones", replace "TROJAN creates a drone ... The Hub sets the drone template and cap (`max_drones`)." with:**

TROJAN creates a drone (5 HP, mini-wheel Shim 3 / Defrag 3) docked on your wheel: on the TROJAN slice itself, else the next free slice clockwise (DEPLOY_DRONE card effects may pick the slice). A drone is fixed to its slice. Its mini-wheel cannot be nudged, spun, respun or changed, and neither can the host be moved with the drone detached. Docking onto an occupied slice replaces the satellite there. The replaced drone is destroyed (placeholder; see Q1). When the slice triggers, the drone triggers too. Enemy Pointer attacks hit a drone on your resolved slice before you. The Hub sets the drone template and cap (`max_drones`). Statuses on a slice stack as ×N (see 2.9) rather than one per slot.

Add: "Only one operative class and specific Firmware or Daemons may move or nudge satellites (they carry the `can_move_satellites` property). Which class is open (see Q1)."

**A6. Insert new subsection 5.2c "Firmware that rewrites tick quality":**

5.2c Calibrated Firmware (example of a new Firmware category)
A Firmware socketed on a slice may raise every sub-tick of that slice one tier (WEAK → GOOD → PERFECT). A 4-tick slice then reads G P P G. This changes what the slice can do, so it belongs under Firmware (6.1), not statuses. Statuses are the opposite lever: TIER_DOWN makes each landing read one tier lower per stack. A Firmware's tick raise and a TIER_DOWN stack apply together and cancel one-for-one.

**A8. Insert new subsection 5.3b "Slice and card Levels":**

5.3b Levels
1. Slices and cards upgrade through Levels I, II, III, IV… (Levels, not tiers: "T1–T4" always means Site tiers).
2. Slice upgrades and card upgrades are separate systems with separate pricing and offers. A slice Level raises that slice's output and may unlock a rider; exact steps are placeholders pending a design pass.
3. A Server Rack may offer a "flash": upgrade a slice one Level or swap a slice. Flash is a row in the data-driven rewards list (see 4.2 and 11), so what is offered, where and when is tunable.
4. Level is displayed on the slice and in the loadout.

**A9. In 5.4 Stationing & Roster, replace the first sentence with:**

Between netruns a surviving operative can be stationed on a Safehouse (a grid-view node, not a netrun node) instead of running; recall any time between runs.

Keep the remainder of 5.4 unchanged, but replace "or rescue them at Terminal events" with "or rescue them at Event sites" (Site names are removed; a Site is its type).

## 3. Contradictions with other sections
- **2.1 (wheel anatomy):** says "6 slices of 5 ticks", slice *i* centred on tick 5*i*, and pointers evenly spaced. Needs variable sizes and pointers on any tick.
- **2.3 / 2.4 (math, tiers):** offset and tier are defined by slice index and fixed 5-tick spans; they need to be defined via cumulative boundaries and the sub-tick map.
- **2.6 (slice types):** needs the BLOCK / SHIELD / SANDBOX renames and SANDBOX = negates one whole attack.
- **2.7 (satellites):** "can be nudged individually" contradicts A5; "Pierce ignores block and shield, not satellites" also needs SANDBOX added.
- **2.9 (statuses):** CORRUPTED self-damage and −1 RAM contradict A4; ×N stacks, OUTPUT_DOWN and TIER_DOWN missing.
- **6.1 (Firmware):** needs the tick-quality Firmware (A6) and `can_move_satellites` chips.
- **6.4 (inner ring):** Rank 1 / 3 inner-ring text depends on the pending inner-ring redesign (G1).
- **Section 3.2 / 4:** Safehouse is listed as a netrun-reachable remote node. Needs alignment with "grid-view node".
- **Section 8.5:** REBEL_CELL elites use "your classes' wheels": variable sizes and Levels must carry over.
- **Section 11 (11.2, rewards):** shop wheel prices 100 / 80 / 100 and flash rows belong there.

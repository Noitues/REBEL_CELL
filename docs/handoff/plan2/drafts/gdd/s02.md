# gdd: section 2 amendment

## 1. Summary

Section 2 gains the new fight-structure rules: phases for every class, threshold parking, enrage, tier-as-base-value, amount modes, listeners, locks, the Parasite ring, and rim gates. The wheel geometry changes: pointers on any tick, variable slice sizes and a per-tick quality map.

- **Statuses:** they become anti-Firmware ×N stacks. CORRUPTED becomes a RAM debuff.
- **Satellites:** fixed to their slice.
- **Defense trio:** BLOCK / SHIELD / SANDBOX.
- **Numbering:** existing subsection numbers 2.1–2.11 are kept so outside references still resolve. New material is added as 2.12–2.15, and 2.11 is retitled.
- **Unchanged:** turn flow, the rewind and preview contract, and the manipulation action set except for locks and gate traversal.
- **Not touched:** corporations, nodes, events, the grid zoom, Heat chip, Site names, G8, G14, D3, D5 and D9. These are outside section 2.

## 2. Amendments

### A1. §2.1 Wheel Anatomy

Replace the first bullet ("A wheel has **30 ticks** … spans ticks 5*i*−2 … 5*i*+2.") with:

> - A wheel has **30 ticks** (12° each) divided into **slices**. Each slot authors a size in ticks (`size_ticks`, 2–7) and a wheel's sizes sum to 30. Odd and even sizes are both allowed. The six-slice cap is lifted (placeholder: enemies 4–8 slices; operative wheels start at 6). A standard slice is 5 ticks, centred on its middle tick, and the default wheel is six standard slices (slice *i* centred on tick 5*i*). Slice sizes and per-tick quality are specified in 2.3 and 2.4.

Replace the "Pointer" bullet with:

> - **Pointer:** a marker (top of the wheel by default). The tick under a pointer decides which slice resolves and how precisely. Pointers may sit on **any tick**; there is no multiple-of-5 rule.

Replace the last bullet ("Enemies may have several pointers, evenly spaced …") with:

> - Enemies may have several pointers at authored ticks, evenly spaced or not. Uneven needles are a design tool: with two needles at ticks 0 and 14, a slice that is Perfect under one needle reads differently under the other, so usually only one can be Perfect. A boss phase may add or move pointers (2.11).

Replace "Satellites: 2–3-slice mini-wheels … docked on an enemy slice (2.7)" with:

> - **Satellites:** 2–3-slice mini-wheels (10 or 15 ticks per slice) docked on a slice, fixed to it (2.7).

Add after the Satellites bullet:

> - **Parasite ring (optional):** a partial outer ring of enemy-owned segments on the player's wheel (2.12).
> - **Rim gates (optional):** marked ticks on a wheel's rim with an effect (2.15).

### A2. §2.2 Turn Flow

In step 1, replace "Player draws to 5 cards … Block from last turn expires." with:

> Player draws to 5 cards, gains +4 RAM (max 12), and the free nudge refreshes. BLOCK from last turn expires. Lock durations tick down (2.5). If the fight is at or past its enrage start turn, the enrage step is applied (2.11).

In step 3, replace sub-steps 1–3 with:

> 1. Defensive slices on both sides (BLOCK, SHIELD, SANDBOX, HOTFIX).
> 2. Offensive slices on both sides (SHIM, OVERFLOW, TROJAN). Parasite segments under your pointers (2.12) resolve in the pass matching their effect; damage-reduction parasites modify your offensive output in this pass.
> 3. Statuses and other effects (INFECT, Daemon hooks, listener reactions to Perfects, 2.13). Then, if an enemy's HP was parked at a phase floor during this resolve, the phase changes (2.11).

Delete "CORRUPTED triggers" from the old step 3; CORRUPTED no longer triggers on resolve (2.9).

### A3. §2.3 Resolution Math

Replace the code block and the line above it with:

```
tick_under_pointer = (rotation + pointer_tick) mod 30      # a Flip rearranges the wheel, see below
slice              = the slice whose range [start_k, start_k + size_k) contains tick_under_pointer
centre_k           = start_k + (size_k − 1) / 2            # half-ticks occur on even sizes
d                  = |tick_under_pointer − centre_k|       # distance from the slice centre
```

Then add:

> Slice ranges are built from cumulative boundaries. Slot 0 begins at tick −⌊size₀ / 2⌋ (placeholder anchor), so a default six-slice wheel is identical to the earlier layout. Nudge, Spin, Snap and Mirror / Shunt operate on these boundaries, not on a fixed 5-tick grid. Snap to Center moves to the nearest tick of d = 0, or to a d = 0.5 tick if the slice has no centre tick.

Append to the Flip paragraph:

> A Flip reverses slice order and each slice's tick quality map. Slice sizes travel with their slices.

### A4. §2.4 Precision Tiers

Replace the table and the following paragraph with:

> Quality is read per **sub-tick**, by distance *d* from the slice centre (2.3):
>
> | Quality | Default rule | Output | Notes |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Perfect | d = 0 | 1.0× | Triggers the class Perfect hook and Perfect-based effects |
> | Good | d ≤ 1 | 1.0× | |
> | WEAK | otherwise | 0.5× | |
>
> Default maps by slice size (left to right): size 2 = G G; size 3 = G P G; size 4 = W G G W (**no Perfect**); size 5 = W G P G W; size 6 = W W G G W W (no Perfect); size 7 = W W G P G W W. A slot may author an override map (`tick_quality`). The loadout screen shows each slice's map.
>
> Firmware can raise every sub-tick of its slice one quality (WEAK → Good → Perfect); a 4-tick slice then reads G P P G. Statuses can lower the read quality (TIER_DOWN, 2.9). Order of application: map, then Firmware, then statuses. A slice that cannot read Perfect cannot fire Perfect hooks.
>
> There is **no miss tier**: every landing is inside some slice. A "miss" means only the **NULL slice** resolving. Effects that mention it (Fault Tolerance, Cold Exit) refer to the NULL slice.

### A5. §2.5 Manipulation

Replace the Nudge row's Cost cell with: "1st per turn free, then 1 RAM (2 RAM when the acting wheel's pointer reads a CORRUPTED slice, 2.9)".

Add after the "Spin resistance" paragraph:

> **Locks.** An enemy source (slice, hub, phase, listener) can apply a **nudge lock** or a **respin lock** to a wheel for N turns; each lock records its source and duration.
> - A nudge lock blocks player nudges on the locked wheel. Cards still work.
> - A respin lock blocks Respin cards and the 4-RAM respin on the locked wheel. The start-of-turn respin is never blocked.
> - Spin, Flip and Freeze are unaffected by either lock.
> - A refused action says why (the lock, its source, turns left), and an overlay marks the locked wheel.
> - Locks refresh each turn only if their source reapplies them.

### A6. §2.6 Slice Types

Replace the DEFRAG, SANDBOX and DETOUR rows with the following. The enum values are kept for saves; only display names and the SANDBOX rule change.

> | Type (display name) | Default target | Effect |
> |---|---|---|
> | DEFRAG, shown as **BLOCK** | Self | Gain block (expires at the start of your next turn) |
> | SANDBOX, shown as **SHIELD** | Self | Gain shield (lingers across turns; capped at 15 for operatives, `shield_cap`) |
> | DETOUR, shown as **SANDBOX** | Self | Negate one incoming attack resolution: every hit of that resolution (one needle's slice trigger) |

Replace the HOTFIX row's Effect with "Restore HP (enemy-facing; amount may be flat or percent)". Add below the table:

> **Defense trio.** BLOCK is temporary, SHIELD lingers, SANDBOX negates a whole attack. Pierce ignores BLOCK and SHIELD, not SANDBOX or satellites.
>
> **Amount modes.** Heal, block, shield and corruption effects carry an `amount_mode`, FLAT or PERCENT. This is a mode of the existing effect, not a status.
> - FLAT amounts on enemy effects are multiplied by the enemy's tier damage factor (2.14).
> - PERCENT amounts are a share of the owner's max HP (heal, block, shield) and are never scaled again.
> - A PERCENT corruption is a share of the target wheel's non-NULL slices (rounded, minimum 1).

### A7. §2.7 Targeting & Satellites

Replace the "Pierce ignores block and shield, not satellites" sentence with: "**Pierce** ignores BLOCK and SHIELD, not SANDBOX or satellites (ruling 2026-09-24, see DECISIONS.md)."

Replace the second bullet's text beginning "Satellites have their own mini-wheel …" with:

> - Satellites have their own mini-wheel and intent and resolve after their host. A satellite is **fixed to its slice**: player actions (nudge, Spin, Flip, Respin, Freeze) cannot move or change its wheel. Exceptions: one operative class, and Firmware or Daemons carrying `can_move_satellites`. Spinning or flipping the host carries docked satellites along.
> - Docking onto an occupied slice **replaces** the satellite there.

### A8. §2.8 Hubs

Append:

> An enemy hub passive may be authored per phase (`hub_override`, 2.11). Hub heal, block and shield may be PERCENT (2.6). A boss's persistent rules are **buffs**, not hub passives, and each buff is turned off by its own Exploit (2.11); Hub Breach remains a card effect with a one-turn duration.

### A9. §2.9 Statuses

Replace the table and add the rules below.

> Statuses are **anti-Firmware**: they change how a slice performs, never what it does. A slot holds statuses as {status: stacks}.
>
> | Status | Rule |
> |---|---|
> | CORRUPTED | A card or nudge acting where the acting wheel's pointer reads a CORRUPTED slice costs **2× RAM** (`corrupted_ram_cost_multiplier`, placeholder). It deals no damage. Does not stack. Lasts until cleansed or combat ends. |
> | OVERCLOCKED | 1.5× output on the slice's next trigger, then the slice becomes CORRUPTED. (Burner Firmware is permanent and adds Heat instead.) Does not stack. |
> | ENCRYPTED | Each stack absorbs the next status applied to that slice (cap 2, placeholder). |
> | OUTPUT_DOWN | −25 % output per stack (cap 3, placeholder). The former PARASITE status is OUTPUT_DOWN ×2. |
> | TIER_DOWN | Each landing reads one quality lower per stack (Perfect → Good → WEAK), applied after Firmware (2.4). |
>
> - A status applies on any landing quality, including WEAK, unless the slice authors a minimum quality.
> - **Statuses survive phase changes.** When a layout changes, each status moves to the new slice covering the old slice's centre tick; stacks landing on the same slice merge. A phase may cleanse on entry (2.11).
> - CORRUPTED's former effects (self-damage, +1 per MAJOR Heat threshold, −1 RAM) are retired. Damage against the operative belongs to the Parasite ring (2.12).
> - CORRUPTED on an enemy slice: effect pending (see section 4 of this amendment).

### A10. §2.10 Preview & Rewind

Insert after the sentence ending "…updated when the wheels settle." (inside the first bullet):

> The preview also shows: **PARKED −N** when a hit would cross a phase floor (2.11); the enrage countdown and the next step; each parasite segment that will trigger and its effect; listener reactions to the staged action (2.13); the RAM forecast including the 2× corrupted cost; and which gates a staged nudge or spin would cross (2.15). Next-phase information is shown unless a boss buff hides it.

Append to the "Rewind" bullet:

> Listener reactions and locks created by an action are undone with it. No rule may use randomness outside a named RNG stream; a parasite's placement is deterministic (2.12).

### A11. §2.11 retitled "Phases, Parking and Enrage"

Delete the existing §2.11 text and replace with:

> **Phases are the unit of fight length.** Every fight has phases, each lasting 3–4 turns (placeholder) and each asking a new question, never plain attrition.
>
> | Class | Phases | Target fight (turns, placeholder) |
> |---|---|---|
> | Normal | 1 | 3–4 |
> | Elite | 2 | 6–8 |
> | Mini-boss | 2–3 | 6–12 |
> | HQ boss | 4–5 | 12–20 |
>
> Each enemy is authored directly; there is no shared boss template.
>
> **Transitions.** Health triggers phases. A phase entry may change the wheel layout (`wheel_override`), hub (`hub_override`), pointers, and may cleanse on entry (`cleanse_on_enter`: NONE / OWN / PLAYER / BOTH). The new layout is revealed on entry only, unless a buff hides it. Pointer changes are:
> - **Multiply** (new pointers at authored ticks);
> - **Migrate** (pointers move; telegraphed one turn ahead);
> - **Orbit** (pointers move N ticks per turn; a trail shows future positions).
>
> **Threshold parking.** While a phase is active, the enemy's HP cannot fall below ceil(max HP × the next threshold).
> - A hit that would cross it leaves HP at the floor and the excess is lost, on every loss path (hits, self-damage, parasites, pierce).
> - The phase changes at the end of that resolve; later hits in the same resolve are also lost.
> - The last phase has no floor.
>
> **Enrage.** A turn count never triggers a phase; it raises damage only (not heal or defense).
>
> | Class | Starts (turn) | Every | Step |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Boss, Mini-boss | 10 | 5 | +25 % damage |
> | Elite | 8 | 5 | +25 % damage |
> | Normal | off | n/a | n/a |
>
> Steps are additive (×1.25, ×1.5, …; placeholder). A boss may author its own enrage trigger. The HUD shows a countdown chip.
>
> **Buffs and Exploits.** A boss may carry several strong **buffs**, each turned off by one Exploit. A Grid draws a subset of its corporation's Exploit pool (placeholder: 5), so not every buff is counterable each run (section 4 owns the Exploit rules).
>
> **Finales.** The DISPATCH finale is a multi-fight sequence (Sync Strike), specified in its own pass.

### A12. New §2.12 Parasite Ring

> Parasites are **anti-slices**: anything a slice can do, a parasite does against you.
> - A **parasite segment** is an enemy-owned arc of authored length (placeholder: 4 ticks) on a partial outer ring of the player's wheel. It rotates with the wheel, so nudges, Spins and Flips move it with your slices.
> - When any of your pointers reads a tick inside a segment, it triggers at resolve (2.2). Its effect can be:
>   - damage to you;
>   - a reduction of your damage to the target (percent);
>   - applying or removing a status;
>   - a RAM drain.
> - **Latching.** A LATCH_PARASITE effect places a segment at the first free run of ticks clockwise from its authored anchor. Segments never overlap, and each wheel has a cap (placeholder: 2).
> - **Removal.** Cleanse can remove one segment instead of clearing a slice.
> - Parasite segments never move your slices or change what a slice does.

### A13. New §2.13 Enemy Listeners

> An enemy may carry listeners that react to your actions: ON_NUDGE, ON_CARD_PLAYED, ON_PERFECT, ON_RAM_SPENT, ON_HIT_DEALT.
> - A listener's reaction resolves inside the triggering action (ON_PERFECT during resolve) and takes no random input.
> - Reactions are shown in the preview and undone by rewind.
> - A listener reaction does not trigger other listeners.

### A14. New §2.14 Tier Base Values

> Site tier sets **base values only**:
> - starting HP, authored per tier (`hp_by_tier`; bosses author `hp_bands` by ICE boss-strength zone, e.g. "in this zone, HP is X");
> - starting damage, from the damage table `enemy_damage_by_tier` = ×1.0 / 1.3 / 1.69 / 2.2 for T1–T4 (placeholder).
>
> FLAT enemy amounts (heal, block, shield, damage) are multiplied by the table. PERCENT amounts read the enemy's own max HP and are not scaled again. Buffs and debuffs (×1.25, −25 %, ×0.5) do not depend on tier. A phase's HP budget is the reference player's damage per turn × the target turns per phase, minus that phase's sustain (numbers in section 11).

### A15. New §2.15 Rim Gates

> A **gate** is a marked rim tick on a wheel with an authored effect.
> - A gate is **crossed** when a nudge or spin carries it past a pointer. Flip, Snap, Respin and the start-of-turn respin never cross gates.
> - The effect (e.g. advancing a counter, dealing damage) resolves once per crossing.
> - The preview lights the gates a staged action would cross.
> - Full mechanic: separate design pass (placeholder rule here).

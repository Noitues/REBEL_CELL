# gdd: section 7 amendment

## 1. Summary
Section 7 keeps its core: deterministic graph auto-battles with a planning phase, the four-step resolution loop, 30 steps, DOWN / TAKEN / HOLDS / BREACHED, and the Armory. These rulings change it:
- Raid modifiers R1-R4 (EXPOSED spotlight, encrypted routes, vehicle types, Heat-scaled second wave with an ultimatum) become rules.
- The DOWN / TAKEN rules from G11 are written down: DOWN is a source for no one, TAKEN is corporation territory, neither can launch netruns, and cut-off nodes stay powered.
- Raids are monitored in the grid view (D6, D9). Raid-win rewards and raid-side events are added.
- Sites are no longer named, and seven corporations supply raid content.
- Defense assets and the raid math are untouched. Combat rules (R1-R13) do not apply to raids, since raids are not wheel fights.

## 2. Amendments

1. **In 7.1 Setup Phase, replace** "Threat vectors are revealed along Grid links from corporate Sites. The player places and repositions defense assets on claimed nodes. Some threats freeze or alter links before the raid." **with:**

   "Threat vectors are revealed along Grid links from corporate-held Sites (TAKEN Sites included). The player places and repositions defense assets on claimed nodes. Some threats freeze or alter links before the raid. Setup happens in the City Grid view; during a netrun, a raid interlude opens that view through the same zoom transition used between the grid and the netrun. The grid map shows the Heat chip and a 'RAID AT n' tick for the next Heat threshold that fires a raid."

2. **In 7.1, after the sentence ending** "(HOLDS / DOWN / TAKEN ...), because resolution is deterministic." **insert new subsection 7.1b:**

   "### 7.1b Raid Modifiers
   A raid carries zero or more modifiers. Each is shown in setup, and every projected outcome includes it. Modifiers use no randomness; any choice is made from named RNG streams at raid generation and then fixed.

   1. **EXPOSED.** One claimed node is marked EXPOSED: threats on it deal +50% damage (placeholder). During setup the player may move the mark one link per payment of 2 Heat or 2 Schematics (placeholder). The mark cannot move onto the home server. The player decides to defend it, move it or sacrifice it.
   2. **Encrypted routes.* At Heat 50 or higher (placeholder), each threat's path beyond its first link is shown dashed and the projected outcome is unavailable for those threats. Decrypting costs 1 Schematic per threat, or is free through a Ghost stationed on a node of the raid (placeholder). Decrypting reveals the exact route and restores the projection.
   3. **Vehicle types.** Each threat has a vehicle: GROUND (default); LANDER (enters at a node inside the network, 2 edges per step); FLYING (ignores ICE Lock and Tar Pit); HEAVY (Turret damage halved). The setup view shows the vehicle on every threat. Assets and the Armory are chosen with these in mind.
   4. **Second wave and ultimatum.** From Heat 50 (placeholder), a raid may carry a second wave that begins at step 15 (placeholder). During setup the player is offered an ultimatum: pay Schematics or Heat to cancel the second wave, or accept it for a larger raid reward (placeholder). The ultimatum is the Schematics sink for the raid layer. Accepted second waves use the Heat-scaled strength of 4.3."

   (Fix typo: item 2 heading reads "**Encrypted routes.**".)

3. **In 7.2 Resolution, replace** "3. Assets fire (range in hops, targeting priority); built-in node defenses fire." **with:**

   "3. Assets fire (range in hops, targeting priority); built-in node defenses fire. FLYING threats are not held by ICE Lock or slowed by Tar Pit; HEAVY threats take half Turret damage."

   **And replace** "4. Threats damage the node they're on." **with:** "4. Threats damage the node they're on (EXPOSED nodes take +50%, placeholder)."

4. **In 7.2, replace** "The raid ends when every threat is destroyed or has reached home, or after **30 steps**." **with:** "The raid ends when every threat, including any second wave, is destroyed or has reached home, or after **30 steps**."

5. **In 7.2, replace** "Threats still on a node at the end leave it **TAKEN**." **with:** "Threats still on a node at the end leave it **TAKEN**, and a DOWN node hit again is TAKEN (3.3)."

6. **In 7.2, after the last sentence of the section ("...repair costs and new entry points."), insert new subsection 7.2b:**

   "### 7.2b DOWN, TAKEN and Connectivity
   1. A DOWN node is a source for no one: it grants no bonus, hosts no netrun launch and projects no asset fire until repaired.
   2. A TAKEN node is corporation territory. It cannot launch netruns and is a raid entry point for the corporation.
   3. Nodes cut off behind a TAKEN or DOWN chokepoint stay powered: they keep their bonuses and assets and are not closed down. Their links through the lost node are de-powered, so threats and the player's own routes cannot pass until it is repaired or reclaimed.
   4. A stationed operative on a DOWN or TAKEN node returns to the reserves unharmed (3.3)."

7. **In 7.2b follow with new subsection 7.2c:**

   "### 7.2c Raid Rewards and Events
   Winning a raid (CELL HOLDS) shows a raid-win reward screen. The reward is set by the trigger and modified by accepted ultimatums; strength scales with Heat as before (4.4). Events may fire in the HQ and raid views as well as in netruns; an event choice may set a raid modifier for the next raid, or start an impromptu fight."

8. **In 7.3 Armory & Persistence, replace** "Assets collected during a netrun bank to the **Armory** (max 6) on Server Rack capture or run completion." **with:** "Assets collected during a netrun bank to the **Armory** (max 6) on Server Rack capture or run completion. A Server Rack's reward list is data-driven (4.2) and may offer assets."

9. **In 7.1, replace** "claimed nodes" **wherever the text refers to a Site by name** with "claimed nodes"; no change needed, but the setup and summary views label Sites by type (Event, Normal Fight, Elite Fight, Mini-Boss Fight, Boss Fight) rather than by name. Only the corporation, the HQ building and the boss keep names. Raid content (threats, assets, triggers) is authored per corporation for all seven corporations (the five existing plus Aurelian Concord and Tethys Deep) alongside the shared set.

## 3. Contradictions with other sections
- **3.3** says a TAKEN node needs a Reclaim; it does not state "DOWN is a source for no one" or powered cut-off nodes. Align with 7.2b.
- **3.1 / 4.1:** "Raids can pass through" unclaimed cleared Sites. Confirm against de-powered links in 7.2b.
- **4.3 / 4.4:** the Heat table's raid thresholds need the "RAID AT n" chip and the second-wave Heat gate (Heat 50) written in. Retaliation text should mention ultimatums.
- **8.3-8.5:** the corporation list needs Aurelian Concord and Tethys Deep for raid names (Collections for Solace; the others need names). Raid names are placeholders.
- **11.x:** the Schematics economy needs the ultimatum sink and the EXPOSED move cost.
- **12:** raid generation uses a named RNG stream; the modifier choices must be checkpointed.
- **9:** the raid and grid view are reached by the zoom, not by a separate GRID VIEW toggle.

# gdd: section 11 amendment

## 1. Summary

- **Scaling rewritten (11.6).** Enemy HP is authored per tier and checked against class bands. Boss HP is chosen by ICE zone. One damage table replaces the three conflicting multipliers. A phase-budget rule, enrage defaults and flat/percent amount rules are added.
- **Fight-length targets replace attrition (11.8).** Targets are now per class, in phases.
- **Central Server rewritten (11.7).** Boss buffs, each turned off by a named Exploit; Exploits are access keys with their own buildings; 7 corporations.
- **Corruption (11.3).** It becomes a RAM cost multiplier.
- **Shop and economy (11.1, 11.2, 11.4, 11.5).** The shop wheel gets its price list and a purchasable nudge. New sinks and sources are added: Workshop, Rack flash, the Exploit building, the raid ultimatum and the Heat Sink node. The reward list becomes data-driven.
- **ICE (11.9).** Boss entries are rewritten.
- **Unchanged:** the Schematics-per-Rack and raid-win table (the 1.7 growth is retained), the RAM income and cap, most Cycle income, the pacing targets for netruns and raids, and the Heat meter itself.
- **Not touched:** text 1.0 mouse/keyboard scope, Site-name removal (this section only names the boss), the zoom between grid view and netrun (D6), and Sync Strike (it has no economy numbers yet).

### A6. 11.6 Scaling (full replacement)

Replace the whole subsection with:

> ### 11.6 Scaling
> **Rule.** The tier sets **base values only**: starting HP, starting damage and rewards. Every mechanic below reads those bases.
>
> 1. **Enemy HP** is authored per tier (`hp_by_tier`, four entries), not multiplied. The validator checks each value against its class band. Values are for T1 / T2 / T3 / T4 (placeholder):
>
> | Class (phases) | T1 | T2 | T3 | T4 |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | Normal (1) | 28–36 | 34–44 | 44–56 | 56–70 |
> | Elite (2) | 56–72 | 68–88 | 88–112 | 112–144 |
> | Mini-boss (2) | 56–72 | 68–88 | 88–112 | n/a |
> | Mini-boss (3) | 85–105 | 104–128 | 132–162 | n/a |
> | HQ boss (4–5) | n/a | n/a | n/a | sum of phase budgets |
>
> 2. **Phase budget.** A phase's HP = (`reference_dpt[tier]` × the number of enemy needles the player's hits land on − the phase's sustain per turn) × `target_turns_per_phase`. `reference_dpt` = [9, 11, 14, 18]; target = 3.5 turns (placeholders). Example, Genome Core P3: (2 × 18 − 6) × 3.5 ≈ 105. More enemy needles mean more HP, by the pointer rule (2.7).
> 3. **Boss HP** is chosen by ICE band, not multiplied: each boss authors `hp_bands`, pairs of {boss-strength ceiling, HP}. Genome Core T4: ≤ 1.00 → 300, ≤ 1.15 → 330, ≤ 1.30 → 360, > 1.30 → 400.
> 4. **Enemy damage** per tier is a single table: `enemy_damage_by_tier` = [1.0, 1.3, 1.69, 2.2]. This replaces the former 1.2^(t−1) here, the 1.3 in the config and the 1.6 schema default.
> 5. **Amounts.** Flat enemy amounts (heal, block, shield, hit counts) are multiplied by the enemy's damage factor. Percent amounts read the enemy's own max HP and are never scaled again. Buffs and debuffs (×1.25, −25 %, ×0.5) do not depend on tier.
> 6. **Amount modes.** Heal, block, shield and corruption exist as FLAT and PERCENT variants of the same effect, not as new statuses.
> 7. **Phases.** HP thresholds trigger phases. Damage cannot cross the next threshold: a hit that would is parked at the floor and the excess is lost.
> 8. **Enrage.** Turns trigger enrage, never phases. Defaults (start / every / step, additive): boss 10 / 5 / +25 %, mini-boss 10 / 5 / +25 %, elite 8 / 5 / +25 %, normal off. It raises damage only. A boss may author its own trigger.
> 9. **Rewards:** base × 1.7^(tier−1), unchanged. Player damage does not scale with tier.

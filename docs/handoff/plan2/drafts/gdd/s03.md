# gdd: section 3 amendment

## 1. Summary
Section 3 (Profile, Campaign & Network Territory) changes in five areas:
- **Seven corporations**, with Aurelian Concord and Tethys Deep added.
- **Territory rules:** Sites are typed and unnamed. G11 sets the DOWN/TAKEN rules (no launch source, links de-powered, cut-off nodes stay powered). Territory comes from the new procedural city (D3).
- **Exploits:** each access key is an Exploit with its own building (D5).
- **Profile unlocks:** G8 gives every class a unique station bonus. The new corporations extend the ICE rules. Class alternatives are clarified.
- **The grid view** is where the campaign is monitored and netruns are chosen (D6, D9).

Not changed:
- Combat rules, wheel geometry, statuses, parasites, phases and enrage. Those belong to section 2 and to enemy/boss content.
- Node integrity numbers, the Node Loss States table, and the home-server patch cost (all still placeholders).
- Netrun node types (Rest, Workshop, Cache), HQ run length, events, slice upgrades, rewards and the shop. Those belong to section 4, 6 and 11.

## 2. Amendments

**A1. In 3.1 Network Territory, replace** the three bullets with:

- The campaign map is the **City Grid** (Section 4), generated procedurally per campaign (coast, districts, terrain) from the campaign seed. Your network is your **home server** (its internal layout set by the chosen home-server variant) plus **claimed Sites** with remote nodes installed, joined by Grid links.
- A Site is identified by its **type** (Event, Normal Fight, Elite Fight, Mini-Boss Fight, Boss Fight, Heat objective, Reclaim, and so on), never by a proper name. Only the corporation, its HQ building (the HQ netrun) and its boss carry names.
- Clearing a Site with a netrun uses it up and makes it claimable. Installing a node costs Schematics.
- **Launch rule:** a netrun may be launched from any owned node (the home server or an installed remote node that is neither DOWN nor TAKEN) to any unowned Site. Owned means claimed and installed, powered, and not DOWN or TAKEN.
- **Powered links:** a link is powered only while both its endpoints are owned and neither is DOWN or TAKEN. A DOWN or TAKEN node de-powers its links. Nodes cut off behind a DOWN or TAKEN chokepoint stay powered and are not closed down; they still count as territory but can't be reached through that chokepoint.
- Raids start from corporate Sites adjacent to your territory and travel along links. Unclaimed cleared Sites are neutral; raids can pass through them. Raid modifiers are defined in Section 7.
- **Grid view.** The City Grid view is where the campaign is monitored and the next netrun chosen. A campaign session starts in the Grid view. JACK IN zooms from the Grid into the netrun. During a netrun the player may zoom out to the Grid and back in at will, with the same transition. There is no separate Grid-view toggle page. The Grid shows Heat as a chip and the next scheduled raid as a **RAID AT n** tick on the Heat meter (n = the Heat value that triggers it).

**A2. In 3.2 Remote Node Types, replace** the sentence "Adjacency example: a Firewall Relay next to a Safehouse shares the stationed operative's bonus." with:

Adjacency example: a Firewall Relay next to a Safehouse shares the stationed operative's bonus (the bonus is the operative class's own, see 5.2).

**Insert after the table, new subsection 3.2b Exploit Buildings:**

### 3.2b Exploit Buildings
1. **Access keys are Exploits.** Each access key a player earns equals one Exploit (the corporation's Exploit pool, Section 4.1 and 8).
2. **Each Exploit has its own special building** on the Grid. A building belongs to one Exploit, is built once per campaign and uses the same node rules as 3.2 and 3.3 (integrity, DOWN, TAKEN). Its effect is that Exploit's role in the breach (for example, an Exploit that turns a boss buff off, Section 2.11). Costs and integrity are placeholders per building.
3. An Exploit building that is DOWN or TAKEN does not count toward `min_exploits_for_breach` (placeholder: 3). Repair and Reclaim follow 3.3.
4. A Grid draws a subset of the corporation's Exploit pool (`exploits_per_grid`, placeholder 5), so not every boss buff is counterable every campaign. Only drawn Exploits have a building on that Grid.

**A3. In 3.3 Node Loss States, make these edits.**

(a) After the table, replace "A stationed operative on a TAKEN or DOWN node returns to the reserves unharmed." with:

A stationed operative on a TAKEN or DOWN node returns to the reserves unharmed. **DOWN is a source for no one:** a DOWN node can't launch netruns, and neither can a TAKEN node. A TAKEN Site is corporation territory. Links are de-powered as in 3.1.

(b) In the TAKEN row's Recovery cell, replace "A Reclaim netrun (single combat, tiny rewards)" with "A Reclaim netrun (a single Normal Fight, tiny rewards)". Rationale: Sites are typed.

(c) Replace "Cleared and claimed Sites can be **patrolled**: a full netrun for loot, Heat and Rank with no objective" with the same text and add: "Patrols and Reclaims use the same campaign netrun rules as other Sites; HQ runs are never patrolled."

**A4. Insert new subsection 3.3b Grid-View Nodes:**

### 3.3b Grid-View Nodes
Two node types exist only on the City Grid and never as netrun map nodes:
1. **Safehouse:** a station slot (see 3.2); it is not placed inside a netrun.
2. **Heat Sink:** a Grid node that reduces Heat per its own rule (numbers placeholders, 4.3).

Netrun-only node types (Rest, Workshop, Cache) are defined in Section 4.2. Intel, Relay and Ambush are not node types. The Relay remains a remote node (3.2) and is never a netrun shortcut node in normal runs.

**A5. In 3.4 Profile Layer, replace** the first paragraph with:

Permanent unlocks: classes (80 Schematics, spent from the current campaign), class alternatives, corporations, home-server variants, new node types, skins, ICE levels.
- **Class alternatives** keep the same deck family with a different core, or the reverse. **Every class and every alternative has its own unique station bonus** (5.2); alternatives may not share the bonus of their base class.
- **Corporations:** seven. Solace Biosystems, Meridian Freight Systems, Halcyon Civic, Orbital Commons, **Aurelian Concord**, **Tethys Deep** and, as the final unlock, REBEL_CELL. Each has its own ICE ladder, Exploit pool, story paths, enemies, mini-boss, boss and HQ netrun.
- **Unlock order and gating** for Aurelian Concord and Tethys Deep: a placeholder rule, locked with section 8.5 (REBEL_CELL requires clearing every other corporation at ICE 10).

**In the same subsection, replace** "Each corporation's ICE ladder unlocks separately; a new corporation may start at (global best − 5)." with:

Each corporation's ICE ladder unlocks separately; a new corporation may start at (global best − 5). The ladder's boss strength (`BOSS_STRENGTH_PCT`) selects a boss HP band (Section 11), not a flat multiplier.

**Add at the end of 3.4:**

**Campaign saves.** Campaign saves from before the procedural city are dropped. Profile data (unlocks, ICE records) carries over.

## 3. Contradictions with other sections
- **2.7 Targeting & Satellites / GDD 5.2:** station bonuses are listed in 5.2's class table, where Wrecker/alternatives share bonuses (G8). 5.2 must carry unique bonuses and the alternate class table.
- **4.1 City Grid:** "30–40 Sites per corporation" and "Tier chains: each T1 opens exactly one T2" conflict with the procedural city (D3) and with the typed unnamed Sites. "Each T2 Site holds at most one Exploit" conflicts with D5 and `exploits_per_grid` (the Exploit is now a building and a drawn subset).

- **7.1–7.3 Raids:** R1–R4 modifiers and the Heat-scaled second wave with an ultimatum.
- **8.3–8.5:** Aurelian Concord and Tethys Deep need sections, and "Renewal Engine" and other boss names follow the D2 name list.
- **GDD 1.3 structure:** "5 corporations" and the HQ flow text need the 7-corporation count and the zoom (D6).
- **11.x:** the 80 Schematics class price and HP-by-band bosses belong in Economy.

# test plan: N3 Phases on every class, threshold parking, status keep, cleanse on enter

## 1. What is a rule here
Rule-bearing (CLAUDE.md rule 6, unit tests):
- `EnemyData.validate` and `BossPhaseData.validate`: phase-count limits per class, threshold ordering, `cleanse_on_enter` enum.
- `CombatantState` floor field (new, derived from max HP and next threshold).
- `effect_interpreter.deal_hit` plus every other HP-loss path: self-damage, parasite, pierce. Each goes through one clamp helper.
- `combat_resolver._check_boss_phases`: phase change at the end of the resolve, excess lost, last phase has no floor.
- `combat_resolver._apply_wheel_override`: status remap by centre tick, stack merge, `cleanse_on_enter`.
- The PARKED preview data (a model value, not a widget). Preview must equal the result.

View glue (flow test only): the "PARKED −N" chip rendering and phase-line display. The combat scene gets one integration smoke test.

## 3. Cases

**test_phase_validation.gd**
1. test_normal_with_phase_entry_rejected: a normal with 1 phase entry fails, reason "normal_has_phases".
2. test_elite_with_one_phase_entry_accepted.
3. test_elite_with_two_phase_entries_rejected: reason "elite_too_many_phases".
4. test_miniboss_two_and_three_phases_accepted.
5. test_boss_four_and_five_accepted_three_rejected: reason "boss_phase_count".
6. test_thresholds_must_descend: [0.5, 0.68] is rejected, reason "threshold_order".
7. test_threshold_edges_rejected: 0.0, 1.0 and 1.2 are rejected. 0.01 and 0.99 are accepted.
8. test_duplicate_thresholds_rejected.
9. test_cleanse_on_enter_unknown_value_rejected: the int 9 fails, reason "cleanse_mode".

**test_phase_parking.gd** (max HP 100, thresholds 0.68 / 0.33; the floor is ceil(100×0.68) = 68)
1. test_floor_is_ceil_of_max_hp_times_threshold: max HP 99 and 0.68 gives 68 (67.32 rounds up).
2. test_hit_crossing_floor_leaves_hp_at_floor: HP 80, hit 30, result 68.
3. test_hit_landing_exactly_on_floor_not_parked: HP 80, hit 12, result 68, parked amount 0.
4. test_hit_one_above_floor_unchanged: HP 70, hit 1, result 69.
5. test_hp_already_at_floor_takes_zero: HP 68, hit 20, result 68, excess 20.
6. test_self_damage_parks: the enemy self-hits for 10 from HP 70 and ends at 68.
7. test_parasite_damage_parks.
8. test_pierce_parks: pierce ignores block and shield but not the floor.
9. test_multi_hit_slice_parks_once_rest_lost: three hits of 10 from HP 75 leave 68. The state reports 23 lost in total.
10. test_last_phase_has_no_floor: in the final phase HP goes to 0.
11. test_normal_has_no_floor: a normal dies from a lethal hit.
12. test_floor_on_heal_not_clamped: healing above the floor is allowed and the cap is max HP.
13. test_one_hp_max_edge: with max HP 1, the floor is ceil(0.5)=1. Document that it doesn't softlock (the phase changes when HP ≤ floor).

**test_phase_transition.gd**
1. test_phase_changes_when_hp_reaches_floor: the check runs at the end of the resolve, not mid-resolve.
2. test_later_hits_in_same_resolve_lost: pointer 1 parks the enemy, pointer 2 deals 0, and the new phase has not yet begun.
3. test_one_transition_per_resolve: a huge hit can't skip two thresholds. P1→P2 occurs and P3 waits for the next resolve.
4. test_new_floor_set_after_change: after the change, the floor is the next threshold. The last phase has none.
5. test_preview_parked_amount_equals_result: preview N, then resolve, then assert actual HP loss + N == raw damage.
6. test_preview_flags_phase_change: the preview model has `phase_change=true` for the same case.
7. test_preview_with_block_and_shield: raw 30, block 10, parked 5, so the preview shows lost 15 and parked 5.
8. test_phase_applies_wheel_and_hub_override: the layout sums to 30 ticks and the hub swaps.

**test_phase_status_keep.gd**
1. test_status_kept_across_override: CORRUPTED on slice 2 stays.

3. test_two_old_slices_land_on_one_new_merge_stacks: OUTPUT_DOWN ×1 plus ×2 gives ×3 (needs N8; before N8, a bool OR).

**test_phase_determinism.gd**
1. test_same_seed_same_inputs_same_hash: a full boss fight run twice gives an equal state hash.

## 5. Existing tests likely to break
- `unit/test_boss.gd`, `test_boss_telegraph.gd`, `test_boss_save_writer.gd`: HP now stops at the floors, so assertions on exact HP after big hits and the old phase triggers need updating. Wheel overrides no longer wipe statuses.
- `test_statuses.gd`: anything that asserts a status is wiped by `_apply_wheel_override`.
- `test_enemies_m2.gd`, `test_solace_content.gd`, `test_combat_content.gd`: `validate()` limits on phases for normals and elites; grep `phases` and `boss_phases`.
- `test_replay.gd`, `test_simulation.gd`, `test_balance_sim.gd`: golden state hashes change if the floor is in CombatantState.
- `test_preview.gd`, `test_plan1_a2_preview.gd`: the preview dictionary gains new keys, so exact-dict comparisons break.

## 6. Order
Write `test_phase_parking.gd` cases 2–5 first. They are pure integer arithmetic on one helper and prove the floor formula (ceil, exact-floor boundary) before the resolver is touched. Next come the transition cases 1–3 (timing, excess lost), then the preview = result case, which pins the contract the UI depends on.

Q: Does a floor-parked hit still fire on-hit listeners (N6) with the pre-clamp or post-clamp damage?
Q: When a phase's threshold is already above current HP at entry (healing or enrage skipping), should the phase change immediately or at the next resolve?
Q: Should max HP of 1 (or tiny phase gaps under a 1 HP minimum) be rejected by validate?

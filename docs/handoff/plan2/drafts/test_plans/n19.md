# test plan: N19 Sim metrics

## 1. What is a rule here
Rule-bearing (CLAUDE.md rule 6): the metric *collection and aggregation* code in `tools/balance/balance_runner.gd`. This covers:
- per-phase turn counting
- parked-damage accounting (the HP lost to the R3 floor)
- the enrage step reached (from N4's state)
- intake per phase
- listener-fire counts (N6)
- the schema of the emitted JSON that `balance.py` reads
- the pass-criteria arithmetic (mean, p90, heal turns per phase, 3–4 turns per phase)

Metrics must be **observational**. They read combat state and events, never consume RNG, never mutate state, and never change a state hash. Determinism is the main risk.

View glue (flow test only): the `balance.py` CLI argument plumbing, the markdown `CMP_*.md` rendering, and the console progress output. One integration smoke run covers them, with no unit tests.

Out of scope: N3, N4 and N6 behavior themselves. Their own plans cover them. Here they are only fed through fixtures.

## 3. Cases

**test_sim_metrics_phase.gd**
1. `test_single_phase_normal_reports_one_phase_with_total_turns`: a normal that dies on turn 4. Assert phases=[4].
2. `test_elite_two_phases_split_at_threshold_turn`: HP threshold crossed at end of turn 3, kill on turn 7. Assert [3,4].
3. `test_boss_five_phases_sum_equals_fight_turns`: a scripted 5-phase boss. Assert the sum equals total turns.
4. `test_phase_change_turn_belongs_to_outgoing_phase`: the change happens at the end of the resolve. Assert the crossing turn counts in the old phase.
5. `test_player_death_mid_phase_records_partial_phase_flagged_incomplete`: assert the last entry has incomplete=true.
6. `test_zero_turn_fight_reports_empty_phases`: an enemy killed in the start-of-combat hook. Assert phases=[] and no division by zero in the mean.
7. `test_phase_skipped_in_one_resolve_is_not_possible_with_parking`: with R3 floors, a huge hit changes only one phase. Assert one entry per resolve.
8. `test_class_target_flags_use_3_to_4_turns_per_phase`: turns 2, 3, 4 and 5 give in_target false, true, true, false.

**test_sim_metrics_damage.gd**
1. `test_parked_damage_equals_excess_over_floor`: floor at 82 of 300 HP (ceil 246), HP 250, hit 20. Assert parked=16, HP=246.
2. `test_parked_damage_counts_every_loss_path`: deal_hit, self-damage, parasite and pierce. Assert the per-path breakdown sums to the total.
3. `test_second_hit_same_resolve_after_cross_parks_fully`: assert the second hit's whole amount is parked.
4. `test_last_phase_has_zero_parked`: no floor in the last phase.
5. `test_intake_per_phase_sums_unblocked_damage_to_player`: BLOCK 12 against hits 5+5+5 gives intake 3. Assert intake=3.
6. `test_intake_excludes_sandboxed_attack`: assert 0.
7. `test_intake_attributed_to_phase_active_at_resolve`: damage on the crossing resolve goes to the old phase.
8. `test_enrage_step_reached_zero_before_start_turn`: a normal reports step 0.
9. `test_enrage_step_boundaries_boss`: turns 9, 10, 14, 15, 20 give steps 0, 1, 1, 2, 3.
10. `test_enrage_step_elite_starts_turn_8`: turns 7 and 8 give 0 and 1.
11. `test_enrage_off_for_normal_reports_null_not_zero`: assert null, so it isn't confused with 0.
12. `test_heal_turns_per_phase_counts_turns_with_heal_gt_0`: three heal turns in P1. Assert 3 and flag over_limit (limit 1).
13. `test_percent_heal_2pct_of_300_counts_6_per_turn_in_sustain`: assert sustain=6.0 exactly.

## 5. Existing tests likely to break
- `unit/test_balance_sim.gd`: exact-key or row-shape asserts on the runner output. Grep for `keys()` and `size()` on result dicts; switch to a required-keys subset check.
- `unit/test_simulation.gd` and `unit/test_replay.gd`: the state-hash baselines must not move. If they do, collection is leaking into state.
- `unit/test_playtest_tools.gd` and `unit/test_heuristic_strategist.gd`: grep for `balance_runner` and fixed-line stdout parsing.
- `unit/test_suite_integrity.gd`: the manifest count (245) rises by 6.

## 6. Order
First write `test_metrics_collection_does_not_change_state_hash` plus `test_same_seed_same_inputs_same_metrics_json`, against a no-op collector stub. They prove the purity and determinism contract before any metric is implemented; every later metric must pass them. Next write the exact-number parked and enrage tests (`parked_damage_equals_excess_over_floor`, `enrage_step_boundaries_boss`), then the schema tests.

Q: Does the p90 use nearest-rank (assumed here) or interpolation?
Q: Are listener fires counted when the listener's effect is fully absorbed by SANDBOX?

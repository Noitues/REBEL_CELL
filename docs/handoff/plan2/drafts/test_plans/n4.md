# test plan: N4 Enrage

## 1. What is a rule here
**Rule-bearing (unit tests):**
- `EnrageData` (new `enrage_data.gd`): fields `start_turn`, `every_n_turns`, `damage_pct_step`, plus `validate()`.
- The pure step function: `enrage_step(turn)` returns the step count, and `enrage_multiplier(turn)` returns the damage factor.
- Class defaults in `campaign_config.tres` `enrage_defaults`, and the lookup order: authored `EnemyData` enrage, then class default, then off.
- `combat_resolver.start_turn`: stores the step on the enemy's CombatantState, and the multiplier is applied to enemy damage only.
- The preview path: it must read the same multiplier as the resolve path.
- Save and restore of the enrage state, which is derived from the turn counter.

**View glue (one flow test, no unit tests):** the HUD countdown chip (layout, color, tooltip text). Its text comes from a rule-side function, `enrage_chip_state(combat)`, which gets unit tests. The widget itself gets only the flow test.

Arithmetic (R4, additive): the multiplier is 1 + step × pct. With a 25 % step, steps 1, 2, 3 give ×1.25, ×1.5, ×1.75. A boss with start 10 and every 5 reaches step 1 on turn 10, step 2 on turn 15, step 3 on turn 20.

## 3. Cases

**test_enrage_data.gd**
1. `test_step_before_start_is_zero`: start 10, turns 1 and 9 give step 0, multiplier 1.0.
2. `test_step_at_start_turn_is_one`: turn 10 gives step 1, ×1.25.
3. `test_step_every_n_turns`: turns 14 / 15 / 19 / 20 give 1 / 2 / 2 / 3.
4. `test_multiplier_is_additive`: step 3 gives exactly 1.75, not 1.953.
5. `test_zero_step_pct_is_flat`: pct 0 gives ×1.0 at any turn.
6. `test_every_n_one_steps_each_turn`: edge value 1.
7. `test_huge_turn_no_overflow`: turn 10000 gives a finite multiplier.
8. `test_defaults_boss_miniboss_10_5_25`: read the `.tres` and compare.
9. `test_defaults_elite_8_5_25`: same, for elites.
10. `test_defaults_normal_is_off`: lookup returns null.
11. `test_authored_overrides_class_default`: Handler `start_turn` 8 wins over the mini-boss 10.
12. `test_validate_rejects_start_turn_below_1`: error names `start_turn`.
13. `test_validate_rejects_every_n_below_1`: error names `every_n_turns`.
14. `test_validate_rejects_negative_step`: error names `damage_pct_step`.
15. `test_validate_rejects_enrage_on_normal`: the class rule (normals have none), with a named reason.

**test_enrage_combat.gd**
1. `test_enemy_hit_scaled_on_step_turn`: base hit 10 on turn 10 deals 12.5 and rounds the same way as `_scaled`. Pin the rounding with a constant: 10 × 1.25 gives 12 under floor, and the test fixes whichever rounding the code uses.
2. `test_start_turn_sets_step_once`: the step is recomputed on `start_turn` and is idempotent when called twice on one turn.
3. `test_preview_equals_result_every_step`: for steps 0 to 3, compare the preview chip number with the actual HP lost.
4. `test_enrage_ignores_heal`: a hub heal of 2 % is unchanged at step 2.
5. `test_enrage_ignores_block_and_shield`: gain_block 12 is unchanged at step 2.
6. `test_enrage_stacks_with_tier_factor`: T3 flat 5 damage × 1.69 × 1.25. Hand-computed; assert exactly.
7. `test_enrage_not_applied_to_percent_amount`: percent damage amounts read max HP and are not scaled again. This assumes N1 exists; skip with a pending marker until it does.
8. `test_enrage_does_not_hit_player_damage`: player hit output is unchanged.
9. `test_enrage_applies_to_every_pointer`: 2 needles, both scaled.
10. `test_enrage_applies_to_satellite_hits`: a satellite of the enemy gets the host's multiplier. Q1 below covers this.
11. `test_enrage_persists_across_phase_change`: phase change on turn 11 keeps step 1.
12. `test_enrage_independent_of_wheel_override`: `_apply_wheel_override` does not reset it.
13. `test_sandbox_still_negates_enraged_hit`: the evade rule is unchanged.
14. `test_pierce_enraged_damage`: pierce plus step 1 ignores block and applies ×1.25.
15. `test_authored_custom_trigger_coexists`: the boss's own trigger fires and the enrage still steps.
16. `test_chip_state_countdown`: turn 7, start 10 gives "3 turns". Turn 10 gives step 1 with 5 to the next step. Check the function return values.
17. `test_chip_state_hidden_for_normal`: returns the hidden state.

**test_enrage_replay_save.gd**
1. `test_seeded_replay_same_hash_through_step_2`: record 16 turns, replay, compare the state hash.
2. `test_hash_includes_enrage_step`: two states that differ only in step hash differently.
3. `test_no_global_randi_in_enrage`: grep-style assertion against the enrage source, and an RngService stream-counter check showing zero draws.
4. `test_rewind_within_turn_keeps_step`: the step is turn-derived, so undo does not change it.
5. `test_rewind_cannot_cross_checkpoint`: still holds on an enrage turn.
6. `test_undo_clean_after_enraged_preview`: stage a card, preview, undo, and the state hash equals the baseline.
7. `test_save_round_trip_mid_fight_turn_12`: restore gives the same multiplier and the same hash.
8. `test_old_save_without_enrage_loads`: there is no save version change, so the missing field defaults to derived. The test confirms no migration is needed.
9. `test_tie_break_two_enemies_same_turn`: the order of resolving enraged enemies stays content id, then node id.

**test_enrage_hud_flow.gd**
1. `test_chip_shows_and_steps_in_boss_fight`: run to turn 10, assert the chip node exists and its text shows step 1. Use bounded wait.
2. `test_chip_absent_on_normal`.
3. `test_chip_matches_chip_state_function`.

**test_enrage_sweep.gd**
1. `test_all_enemies_resolve_enrage_by_class`: all 57 enemies resolve to normal off, elite 8, others 10.
2. `test_all_authored_enrage_validate`: runs after the content pass lands.

## 6. Order
Write `test_step_at_start_turn_is_one` and `test_multiplier_is_additive` first. They prove the pure formula and the additive-versus-compounding decision (Q1 in the designer questions) before any resolver code exists. Next come the defaults tests (8–10), which pin the `.tres` shape. Then `test_preview_equals_result_every_step`, the riskiest rule.

Q: Do satellites and drones owned by an enraged enemy get the multiplier (assumed yes, case 10)?
Q: Rounding of the enraged amount: floor, round, or ceil? Tests pin one once chosen.
Q: Does a reset or a fresh combat after a Freeze (skipped respin) count as a turn for enrage? Assumed yes, turn counter only.

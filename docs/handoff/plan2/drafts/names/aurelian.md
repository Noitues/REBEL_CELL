## Aurelian Concord

1. **THE GOLDEN LEDGER**: A finance corporation's Central Server that tracks every transaction and contract in the golden concord's ceremonial order; the boss mechanic is a ledger that rebalances itself each turn, redistributing damage across pointers unless Exploits break its accounting symmetry.

2. **THE ACCORD VAULT**: The ceremonial heart of Aurelian's wealth and golden contracts; the boss locks its defenses behind tiered accords (phases keyed to exploit order), with each Exploit breaking a seal in the vault's hierarchical structure.

3. **THE AUREATE THRONE**: The seat of the golden concord's authority, radiating outward through ceremony and ledger; the boss is defended by mirror-pointers representing each tier of the hierarchy, and Exploits dismantle the throne's ceremonial power structure rather than damaging it directly.

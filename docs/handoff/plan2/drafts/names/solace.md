## Solace Biosystems

1. **THE RENEWAL ENGINE**: the boss mechanic hook—Solace's implant subscription model as a literal engine of forced dependency; defeating it means breaking the cycle of mandatory upgrades and kill-switch threats that keep patients enslaved to monthly payments.

2. **THE COMPLIANCE CORE**: the corporation's fiction—a central hub enforcing medical gatekeeping and Compliance Lock resistance; breaching it frees implants from the dose-based control system that turns treatment into a weapon.

3. **THE PATIENT ZERO VAULT**: the disease-is-you premise inverted—Solace treats you as both the disease vector and the product; raiding this server exposes the genetic profiling and pricing discrimination hidden in their "personalized medicine" marketing.

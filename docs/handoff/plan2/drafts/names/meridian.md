## Meridian Freight Systems

1. **THE HUB**: Meridian's routing nexus—the central redistribution point where all shipments converge and diverge. Boss mechanic: The Customs Seal (a Hub defense) must be breached first; until then, The Hub shields itself each turn, forcing you to chip away at customs barriers before damaging the core.

2. **THE LOGISTICS CORE**: The nerve center of Meridian's automated supply chains and tracking systems. Boss mechanic: Enemy Conveyors (orbiting pointers) circle the server in predictable lanes; destroying them one by one weakens The Logistics Core's defenses and slows its turn cycle.

3. **THE SORTING ENGINE**: Meridian's algorithmic heart that prioritizes, routes, and redistributes freight across the network in real time. Boss mechanic: Each turn, the server reorders its pointer lineup (like PRIORITY OVERFLOW) unless you use an Exploit to lock its queue; Intel reveals the next sort cycle, giving you a window to strike.

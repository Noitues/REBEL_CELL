## Pick the theme
- **Meridian** (4 options; full rules in `meridian.md`):
  - A **PAYLOAD**: loot has weight; carried weight turns WEIGHT (spin resistance) on your own wheel. Cheapest; recommended.
  - B **CUSTOMS**: checkpoints make you surrender an item (returned after the run).
  - C **DELIVERY WINDOW**: finishing fights fast pays a bonus. Lightest, weakest identity.
  - D **SUPPLY LINE**: the Grid becomes feeder chains you cut to weaken the boss. Strongest identity, largest cost.
- **Orbital** (4 options; full rules in `orbital.md`):
  - A **DEAD ZONE** (blackout, your leaning): on dark turns the free nudge is locked; dark raid nodes can't be re-ordered; relays escape it.
  - B **OVERWATCH**: an orbital strike telegraphs onto a slice / node, exact and readable. Recommended.
  - C **KESSLER**: debris cascade on the Parasite ring; lands last.
  - D **INTERCEPT**: the enemy reads your orders (shallow).

## What the scrambled run conveyed

I wrote this section from the scrambled sheets only, before opening the README or the worded runs.

**Understood from images alone:**
- **The board.** A tilted neon grid between a dim amber block on the left and a bright cobalt tower on the right. Three lanes are labeled at the right edge.
- **Which side is mine.** The skull (scrambled/011, 012) and the red −1 with a ring (scrambled/006, 007) land on the left block, so that is "mine" and it is under attack from the right.
- **Three unit types.** Cyan triangle, magenta square and orange circle stay consistent across the legend, the board sentries, the beams and the panel rows. This reads without effort.
- **Structure.** A counter shows "x/3", so there are three raids. Every raid runs the same 8 beats in the same order, so the rhythm is learnable.
- **Choose a spot.** Dashed yellow circles marked "+" with numbers (6/10/12) mean "pick one spot, worth this much" (scrambled/000, 010, 020).
- **Three possible attacks, each with a forecast.** Three colored dashed lines run right to left. The panel lists three colored rows, each with a green heart or a red X (scrambled/002, 012, 022).
- **A reveal.** One row turns highlighted and one line goes solid, and a "?" row precedes it. Something hidden became real (scrambled/003–004, 013–014).
- **A fix is offered.** Yellow-green rings appear on sentries next to a list of options with verdict glyphs (scrambled/005, 015, 025).
- **An outcome ladder.** Red X in raid 1, green heart in raid 2, orange broken heart in raid 3 (scrambled/007, 017, 027, 028). A top-bar box turned red after raid 1, so it is a life pip.
- **Risk tiers.** Three cards with 1/2/3 flames, one of them gets picked (scrambled/009 picks the first; scrambled/019 picks the third).
- **A threshold meter.** The orange bar at the top has "50 +1 / 75 +2" ticks.

**Guessed, low confidence:**
- A white swirl on one district in scrambled/009 and 019 meant "targeted fix". I couldn't tell it was a repair.
- Rows like "1 x, 2 y" looked like counts of something good and something bad.

**Not conveyed:**
- The hunter. I never found it as a separate agent.
- Why raid 1 damaged me. I couldn't trace which beam reached home.
- The difference between a ghost and a sentry, and that response options cost Heat.
- What "orange" (battered) costs.
- That districts can be lost.
- What the score terms are. Heat turned out to be the largest one: −26 in scrambled/028.

**Checked against the worded runs afterward:** I got right the type system, the 3-raid leg, which side is home, the per-route forecast and the outcome ladder. I half got the sealed-route mechanic. I missed the hunter, the repair/decay loop and the score cost of Heat.

The README says the lab exists to show that "ownership, the hunter and the beams read clearly". Wordless, two of those three do. Ownership reads: amber vs cobalt, and TAKEN tiles turn blue in breach/020–021. Beams read. The hunter does not.

## Findings

### P1

1. **"SAFE" right before a guaranteed breach.**
   - In breach/018 and 019, all three intensity cards (LOW/NORMAL/HOT) show green SAFE, under a red "Next HOME hit ends the campaign" banner.
   - Next is breach/020 DECAY: MID 0 and SOU 0 are TAKEN, and both sentries in them return.
   - Then at breach/022, every candidate route forecasts BREACHED. In breach/023 and 026, every response option is BREACHED too.
   - Likely cause: the verdict is computed before decay and before placement. Whatever the cause, the player committed on a false reassurance.
   - Scrambled/008 and 018 show all-green cards as well. If SAFE is always green, it tells the player nothing.
2. **The lethal choice is presented as a routine line.**
   - At breach/019, the panel's answer is a small green line: "PROTECTS HOME: build ENFORCER, repair MID 0", the same weight as routine lines.
   - The naive line repaired SOU 2 (−6) instead, built nothing, and ran NORMAL. That was the decisive mistake.
   - After that, raid 3 is eight frames with no real agency (breach/022–029).
   - The decision that mattered got the same visual weight as "Leg value after this step".

### P2

3. **The hunter is not findable in stills.**
   - It appears only as panel text: "HUNTER: the street of the first touch" (planned/006, 016, 026; breach/006).
   - On the board I can't separate the hound from sentry glows.
   - This is the lab's own stated goal. It needs checking in motion, but nothing on the board marks its street before launch.
4. **The response menu answers itself.**
   - Rows carry their final verdicts: planned/005 (3× HOLDS), planned/015 (one green row of four), planned/025 (only JAM holds).
   - Options with the same result are merged, e.g. "HOLD / REDEPLOY (6 ways) / DEPLOY (2 ways)" in planned/025. That's honest, but it shows the choice space is 2–3 outcomes wide.
   - The mid-launch beat becomes "pick the green row". This is the S ceiling.
5. **Some reveals carry no stakes.**
   - In planned/002, all three candidates HOLD, so the reveal in planned/004 cannot matter.
   - In breach/023, all three are BREACHED, so it cannot matter either.
   - About half the raids in these two legs have a reveal with nothing riding on it.
6. **Intensity is nearly score-neutral, and lines converge on Heat.**
   - Bank and Heat both score 1:1 (planned/028: bank +15, Heat above 44 −26).
   - The cards trade income against Heat at roughly that ratio: LOW +3/+6 and HOT +10/+18 (planned/008); HOT +21/+18 in scrambled/019.
   - The careful line spent Heat on ghosts (+5, planned/001 and 021) and spoofs (+2, planned/017 and 027). The scrambled line spent it on HOT. Both ended at Heat 70 and −26.
   - The real stake is the 50/75 beam thresholds, which the cards show only as tiny "H55/H67" numbers.
7. **A HOME pip is cheap until it's fatal.**
   - Losing a pip costs −8 (scrambled/028), less than 8 Heat. The third pip ends the campaign (breach/029).
   - The score teaches the player to shrug off the first two hits.
8. **Launching into red forecasts has no friction.**
   - breach/002 shows all three routes DAMAGED, with the same LAUNCH button as a green forecast. The naive line launched into certain damage twice (breach/002, 012).
9. **"Battered" has no mark on the board and no stated cost.**
   - DAMAGED gets a skull on HOME (planned/011–012). BATTERED gets nothing on the board (planned/021–022).
   - planned/027 reads "HOME 3→3", so the cost of being battered is unclear.

### P3

10. **Plan and forecast boards are cluttered.** Three dashed typed lines, arrows and markers overlap, and grey blocks hide them (planned/011, 021; breach/011, 022).
11. **Text overlaps and gets clipped.**
    - "DEPLOY (2 wa…" runs under "BATTERED" in planned/025, and rows overlap in scrambled/025.
    - The decay lines are cut off ("…its DRONE sentry ret…") in breach/020 and 021.
12. **Key numbers are tiny.** The Heat thresholds ("50 +1 / 75 +2") and the "LEG VALUE NOW" formula in the top bar are very small. Yet the leg value swings hard, 32→18→−2 (breach/007, 018).
13. **The ghost/spoof kit doesn't explain itself.** The kit reads "GHOST 2 / SPOOF 2", but the spoof's role (paying for MOVE/JAM, +2 Heat) is only explained inside the response header (planned/005).

## Three changes that would raise the score most

1. **Honest, causal between-raid verdicts** (P +1.5, D +0.5, C +0.5).
   - Compute each card's verdict after decay, as "best line next raid: HOLDS / DAMAGED / BREACHED".
   - Promote the "PROTECTS HOME" line to a red-bordered requirement that states the consequence, e.g. "skip repair MID 0 → raid 3 BREACHED".
   - Add friction to LAUNCH when every candidate route is red.
2. **Put the verdicts on the board** (N +1.5, W +0.5, V +0.5).
   - Stamp each candidate route's glyph (heart / broken heart / skull) where it ends at HOME.
   - Draw the hunter's street with a hound mark before launch.
   - Give DOWN districts a "TAKEN next raid" countdown mark, and battered districts a cracked mark.
   - Thin out the plan lines.
3. **Make the leg economy carry decisions** (D +1.5, C +0.5, S +0.5).
   - Charge Heat in bands at the 50/75 thresholds instead of a flat −1 per point.
   - Price HOME pips well above Heat.
   - Let each intensity buy something different (e.g., HOT gives an extra capture, LOW gives a sentry), so neither LOW nor HOT is the default.
   - Optional fourth change: decide the hunter's street at the response step instead of from the first touch, keeping one live unknown during the reveal (S +2, P −1).

All three together come to roughly +6 on /50, about **40–41**: above Invisible, Inc. on the total, but still well below it on S.

## Ceiling: rules or presentation?

Both, split by axis.

- **Presentation and one bug (fixable):**
  - The P loss is mostly the SAFE bug plus underweighted advice.
  - The N and V losses come from what the board fails to show: hunter, decay, battered.
  - Fixing these gets the lab into the low 40s.
- **Rules (the ceiling above ~41):**
  - The only in-raid choice comes after a reveal into a menu where every outcome is known. So the reveal can surprise you, but your answer to it cannot.
  - Score-neutral intensity and a flat Heat tax flatten the leg: a careful line and a sloppy one both ended at Heat 70.
  - Reaching Spire or ITB depth needs more simultaneous pressure, for example two sealed beams, or a response that must trade a district for HOME.
  - Without that, the mode stays a well-labeled puzzle with S around 5.

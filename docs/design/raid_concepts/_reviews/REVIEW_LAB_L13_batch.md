# Review: Raid Lab (city raid), batch of scrambled / planned / breach captures

**Verdict: 34/50. The forecast is clear and the consequences are real, but late raids collapse into "pick the least-bad row", and every decision panel is unreadable without words.** It lands between Kingdom Rush and FTL. S 6, V 6, Fit 7.

> Method: I read the three `scrambled/` sheets before opening the README or the worded runs. The scrambled section below records only what I took from the images. Stills cannot show motion, so S is judged from the beat sequence, not from animation.

## What the scrambled run conveyed (images only, written before the README)

**Read without words**
- **Board layout:** an amber, staircase-like home HQ on the left and a lit cobalt tower on the right, with three lanes or routes between them and four lettered columns. Threats come from the right toward home.
- **Capture:** yellow circles with `+` and numbers mark claimable plots, and the numbers read as value. One claim in raid 1 and two later suggests a perk (scrambled/000, /011, /012).
- **Typed matching:** the cyan triangle, pink square and orange circle sentries match the beam colors. I guessed "a sentry stops its own color" from color alone (scrambled/001–/003).
- **Outcome glyphs:** a full cyan heart means fine, a broken amber heart means hurt, and a red skull ring on the home HQ means home is threatened (scrambled/013, /014). AFTERMATH repeats the glyph large (scrambled/008, /020).
- **Reveal and choice:** the panel walks rows with a highlighted "current" row (scrambled/003→/004). Glowing yellow-green rings around sentries during RESPONSE read as "you may act now" (scrambled/005).
- **Hunter:** a diamond, then a hound glyph, travels down one column (scrambled/006, /007).
- **Escalation:** the Heat bar climbs to its 50 and 75 ticks, and beams grow from 1 to 2 to 3 per raid (scrambled/003, /015, /027).
- **Intensity:** the between-raids cards carry 1, 2 and 3 flames, which reads as "risk dial".
- **Record:** three colored rows with heart or broken-heart badges give a per-raid recap (scrambled/033).

**Not conveyed**
- Which route is sealed versus known. The `?` icons hint at uncertainty but do not say "one of these three".
- What the hunter does, or why two candidate streets are shown.
- What each RESPONSE option does (scrambled/005, /017, /029). Only the result glyphs survive scrambling; the action is text-only.
- What intensity trades. Flames say "more", but the cost (Heat, captures, repair) is text, and the green badge on all three cards is identical (scrambled/009, /021).
- Why raid 1 ended BATTERED (scrambled/008) when the forecast panel showed mostly hearts.
- That Heat is a score penalty at the end (scrambled/033 right panel).

## Findings

### P1

**1. Late-leg decisions collapse.**
- In the careful line at Heat 92, every forecast route ends BATTERED except one MID case that needs a JAM (planned/026).
- After NORTH is revealed, all 14 response rows read BATTERED/BATTERED (planned/029). The "response" is cosmetic.
- The breach line is worse: every HOLD row says BREACHED, and only SACRIFICE rows survive (breach/025, /026, /029).
- Separately, the forecast prints `best response: …` on every route (planned/002, /014, /026; breach/002, /013). The game solves itself. P is excellent, but D is capped.

**2. The safety copy contradicts the table.**
- breach/025 and breach/026 show a red box, "HOME is at 1/3: one hit ends the campaign", and then green text, "This plan keeps HOME on every route."
- Every row above that text reads BREACHED. The line means "a sacrifice response exists". As written, it reassures a player who is about to lose the campaign.
- The naive line launched and held, and the run ended (breach/032).

**3. Decision panels are text-only.**
- With scrambling, the RESPONSE list (scrambled/005, /017, /029) and the BETWEEN intensity cards (scrambled/009, /021) can only be decoded through their result badges.
- The board does not preview the action of the highlighted row: which sentry moves, or which district is sacrificed.
- The `HOLDABLE: a plan exists` badge appears on all three intensity cards every time (planned/009, /021; breach/020), so it carries no information.

### P2

**4. Raid 1 can be inert.**
- planned/004 and /006 show "Nothing touched", the hunter misses, and the result is HOLDS with no response needed (planned/008).
- A third of the leg passes without tension in the careful line.

**5. The hunter beats are near-duplicates.**
- `hunter_reveal` and `sealed_hunter` are almost identical stills (planned/006 vs /007, /018 vs /019; breach/030 vs /031).
- Each raid spends about 11 beats, and two of them say the same thing.

**6. The late board is cluttered.**
- Three beams, a hunter path, ghosts, sentries and 0–3 column labels overlap (planned/027, /030; scrambled/030).
- District tags (`NOR 2`, `SOU 0`) are tiny, and the heart and broken-heart markers sit at the right edge, far from the plots they describe.

**7. Heat greed is under-signposted.**
- The greedy line ran HOT twice and lost 37 points to Heat at the end: −25 above 44 and −12 for bands (planned/033).
- The intensity cards show projected Heat (`H55/H60`) but not the score cost. That cost appears only in the small header line.

### P3

**8. The response list scrolls.** In planned/029 and breach/029 ("rows 1-6 of 14", "1-6 of 7"), options sit off-screen during the key moment.

**9. Labels are clipped and abbreviated.** `NETRUN`, `ENFORC`, `st`, `A/B` and `(v/2, free on NORMAL)` slow the read on every frame.

**10. Docs do not match the build.** The README describes "L8" and its Run section is empty, while this is filed as an L13 batch. That is a minor documentation mismatch.

**What works:**
- Decay is excellent. breach/020 shows "repair or lose it" with one repair allowed, and breach/022 turns the district into a blue TAKEN tile. It is wordless and it persists.
- The 1-HOME launch friction is the right instinct (breach/026, "LAUNCH ANYWAY"). The bug is only its copy.

## Three changes that would raise the score most

1. **Keep raid 3 a real choice, and stop printing the answer.** (D +2, S +1)
   - Make the generator guarantee that each raid's response menu has at least two distinct outcome classes. For example, Heat escalation could also grant one extra reserve sentry or spoof.
   - Show the per-route outcome table but hide `best response`. Alternatively, show it only on request at a Heat cost.
2. **Make decisions wordless: preview on the board, not in the list.** (N +2, W +1)
   - Hovering a RESPONSE or intensity row should draw its effect on the board: a sentry ghost moving, a sacrificed plot crossed out, extra beams for HOT.
   - Replace `HOLDABLE` with each card's worst-case glyph (heart, broken heart, skull) and its Heat score cost.
3. **Fix the copy and the clutter, and merge the beats.** (P +1, V +1, S +0.5)
   - Rewrite breach/025 as "Your plan breaches on every route. Only SACRIFICE after the reveal saves HOME."
   - Merge `hunter_reveal` and `sealed_hunter` into one beat.
   - Fade beams that are resolved or not yet active, and pin outcome glyphs to the plots they affect.

With all three, I would expect about 40/50, landing between Invisible, Inc. and Slay the Spire.

---

## Ceiling: rules or presentation?

**Both, but the hard part is in the rules.**
- **Presentation:** W, N and V are limited by presentation. Board previews, glyph badges and decluttering could recover about 3–4 points without touching the rules.
- **Rules, D:** the gap to the ITB/StS tier is a rules ceiling. Three routes times two hunter streets is a fully enumerable space, so the game can and does solve it for you. Heat escalation also outpaces the player's tools by raid 3, so the best play degrades from "find the hold" to "choose how to be battered".
- **Rules, S:** suspense is capped the same way. When every row shares one result, the reveal has nothing left to decide.
- **What would lift the ceiling:** more per-raid levers (deck-fed tools, partial information on the hunter) or escalation that adds options alongside threats. Polish alone will plateau around 37–38.

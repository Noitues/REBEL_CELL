# Review: Raid lab (city raid) batch, scrambled / planned / breach

**Verdict:** A highly predictable, honest forecasting machine on a readable neon board. In all nine captured raids, though, the "sealed beam" never changes an outcome, so the reveal is ceremony and the leg is decided at the plan step. **34/50 · S 4 · V 6 · Fit 7.**

> Method note: I read the scrambled sheets first and wrote section 3 before using the README or the readable sheets. Stills cannot show motion. S is judged only from the beat sequence and the state changes between frames.
> Housekeeping: the README and HUD say **L8** ("L8 CITY RAID"), but this file is filed as L9. I assume it is the same lab.

## 2. Placement

Ranking: Kingdom Rush 33 < **this lab 34** < FTL 35 < Invisible, Inc. 39 < Slay the Spire 42 < Into the Breach 45.

- **Against Kingdom Rush (33; S 8, V 8):**
  - The lab wins on P and C. You know the exact cost of a raid before you commit, and the damage carries across the leg.
  - It loses badly on S and V and probably on N. KR's lane threats read from silhouettes; here they read from panel text.
- **Against FTL (35; S 8, V 7):**
  - The lab wins clearly on P, since FTL never tells you the result of a jump.
  - It loses on S (4 vs 8) and on the felt weight of D. FTL's per-event choices create drama that HOLD/JAM rows do not.
- **Against Into the Breach (45; S 5):** this is the true design neighbor, with perfect information and a telegraphed threat.
  - The lab is already near ITB on P.
  - It is roughly 3 points behind on D, because ITB's responses actually rearrange the outcome.
  - It is roughly 3 behind on N, because ITB's threat glyphs need no text.
  - Its S (4) is also below ITB's 5. ITB at least shows what each push will do. Here even the hidden element is inert.

## 3. What the scrambled run conveyed (images only, before the README)

**Understood without words:**

- **The board.**
  - A city strip between an amber base on the left and a blue tower on the right, with three horizontal lanes and columns numbered 0 to 3.
  - The board labels NORTH/MID/SOUTH and all numerals survived scrambling, which softens the test.
- **Each raid runs as a fixed sequence of panels:**
  - pick a glowing plus-shaped district with a value (scrambled/000)
  - place colored pieces near the base (001)
  - see three candidate cards (002)
  - watch beams come in from the right (003–006)
  - see a big emblem (007)
  - get a three-column choice screen (008/009)
- **Color is type.** Cyan triangle, magenta square and orange circle pieces match beams of the same color. I guessed that a same-color piece stops a same-color beam.
- **There are three raids.** The counter reads 1/3, 2/3, 3/3, and the record (028) has three rows that each end in the same shield emblem.
- **The left bar is some pressure stat.** It climbs from 44 to 50 to 68 to 70. The between-raid columns add +6, +12 or +18 to it, so I read them as "safe / normal / greedy."
- **The three amber pips at the top stayed full,** so I read them as base health that never got hit.

**Not conveyed:**

- **What the "?" row in the launch panel is.** In scrambled/003 I could not tell that one of the three cards is the hidden real route.
- **The hunter.** I did not notice it at all. In scrambled/006 the hunter beam looks like just another cyan beam.
- **The ghost and spoof kit.** The dotted circle and the lime text meant nothing.
- **Hits versus blocks.** The yellow ticks on the board had no clear meaning.
- **That base damage can lose the campaign.** No damage occurred, so the run never showed its strongest wordless signal, the skull and -1 seen in breach/001 and breach/006.
- **Why raid 3's emblem changed from a shield to a bolt** (scrambled/022, 025). Both are amber, so I could not tell whether the change was good or bad.
- **Why the final number (59) is what it is.**

**Net:** the scrambled run teaches the phase rhythm and color matching. It does not teach the stakes.

## 4. Findings

### P1

- **P1-1 · The sealed beam never matters (S, D).**
  - Every forecast panel in the batch shows all three candidates with the same outcome tier:
    - HOLDS ×3 in scrambled/002, scrambled/012, planned/002 and planned/012
    - BATTERED ×3 in scrambled/022 and planned/022
    - DAMAGED ×3 in breach/002 (South even says "no response changes this")
    - in breach/012 two are DAMAGED and one is BATTERED, but HOME still drops either way
  - So the reveal frames (\*/004, \*/014, \*/024) carry no information that changes the decision.
  - Response then collapses to HOLD when safe (planned/005, planned/015) and JAM when battered (planned/025, scrambled/025).
  - The game's signature mystery is doing no work. Suspense ends at the forecast step.
- **P1-2 · The breach itself is missing, and so is any lethal warning (C, W).**
  - The breach sheet stops at breach/019_r2_between_picked with HOME 1/3, Heat 68 and leg value 2. There is no raid 3, no breach and no campaign-loss screen, so I cannot score the most important consequence in the mode.
  - The between screen at HOME 1/3 (breach/018, 019) shows red pips but no "next HOME hit loses the campaign" line.
  - The naive line spends 6 to repair SOU 2 instead of building a sentry. Nothing on screen frames that as the fatal choice.
- **P1-3 · A doomed plan is fully telegraphed, but rescue is impossible once committed (D).**
  - breach/001 and breach/002 show a skull and "DAMAGED" on all three routes before launch, which is excellent P.
  - Yet both response rows in breach/005 and breach/015 are also DAMAGED, including "REDEPLOY (3 ways)."
  - Committing a bad plan is unrecoverable. Combined with P1-1, the entire raid is decided at PLAN. That is a legitimate ITB-style choice, but then the 5 post-launch beats are presentation, not play.

### P2

- **P2-1 · HOLDS and BATTERED look alike (N).** Both are amber icons: the shield in planned/017 and the bolt in planned/022. Only DAMAGED gets red and an X. Without words, "fine" and "your districts got hit" are the same color.
- **P2-2 · Amber plus-highlights mean two things (N).**
  - Capture candidates (breach/010) and DOWN-district repair targets (breach/018) use the same amber plus-shape.
  - The "picked" yellow ring (breach/009, scrambled/019) is a third use of the same hue.
- **P2-3 · REDEPLOY is a trap or noise (D).** Every REDEPLOY row shown is worse than HOLD or JAM: MOVE DRONE DAMAGED in planned/015, MOVE NETRUNNER DAMAGED in planned/025, all three DAMAGED in breach/015. It adds rows without adding choices.
- **P2-4 · Plan and forecast boards are hard to read (V, N).** planned/021, planned/022 and scrambled/021 show 5–8 crossing colored beams plus block ticks, and the "on the board" preview route is hard to pick out.
- **P2-5 · The hunter has no identity (N, W).**
  - It takes the color of its type: cyan in planned/006, pale or white in breach/006.
  - The hound marker is tiny, and "the street of the first touch" is only explained in text.
- **P2-6 · Intensity may under-charge aggression (D).** This compares different legs, so treat it as a hedge, not a result.
  - The scrambled line picked HOT in raid 2 (scrambled/019, bank 27) and still scored 59, with Heat −26 against bank +27 and districts +52 (scrambled/028).
  - The careful leg-5 line ran LOW twice and scored 46 (planned/028).
  - If that holds on equal seeds, Heat is not a real brake.

### P3

- **P3-1 · "Leg value now" jumps are unexplained.** It goes 38 → 33 in planned/001 → 007 and 32 → 18 in breach/001 → 007, and the board gives no breakdown.
- **P3-2 · The scrambling leaks words.** Board labels (NORTH/MID/SOUTH) and digits survive, so the N test is gentler than it appears (scrambled/000 onward).
- **P3-3 · The Heat threshold ticks ("50 +1 / 75 +2") are tiny.** They are also the only place that explains "off (at Heat 50)" in the plan panel (planned/001).
- **P3-4 · Panel text is small at contact-sheet resolution,** and the right panel carries all the stakes. The board alone never says "this will cost you."

## 5. The three changes that would raise the score most

1. **Make the sealed candidates diverge (rules): S +2, D +2.**
   - Constrain the generator so that at least one or two raids per leg have candidates spanning two or more outcome tiers.
   - The response choice (JAM vs REDEPLOY vs HOLD) should then decide the result after the reveal.
   - That gives the reveal frames real stakes and gives REDEPLOY a reason to exist.
2. **Build a wordless outcome and stakes language (presentation): N +2, W +1, V +1.**
   - Give each tier its own shape and color: HOLDS green/cyan shield, BATTERED amber cracked shield, DAMAGED red X, BREACH skull.
   - Use a separate highlight for repair targets (for example a red wrench outline) versus capture.
   - Add a lethal marker on the last HOME pip.
   - Give the hunter a distinct silhouette and trail color regardless of its type.
3. **Show and frame the breach (presentation plus capture): C +1, W +1, S +1.**
   - Capture breach raid 3 through to campaign loss.
   - Add a between-raids warning at HOME 1/3 ("next HOME hit ends the campaign").
   - Make the repair-versus-build trade explicit at that moment.

Together these would plausibly take the lab to about **40–41/50 with S around 6–7**, placing it between Invisible, Inc. and Slay the Spire.

## 6. Rules ceiling or presentation ceiling?

**Both, split by axis.**

- **N, V and part of W are presentation-capped.** The information already exists in the panels and only needs glyphs. That is roughly +3 to +4 available without touching rules.
- **S and D are rules-capped.**
  - With a perfect forecast and uniform candidates, the raid is a solved puzzle at PLAN.
  - No amount of beam animation will make reveal frames like planned/004 tense when the card already said HOLDS ×3.
  - The lab's P of 9 is earned partly by having nothing left to hide.
- **The path past about 38 is a rules change:** hidden information that splits outcomes, plus response options that matter. Presentation polish alone tops out around there.

# Tethys Deep: boss wheel ideas (for the Boss Wheel v2 agent)

Boss wheels moved to `docs/concepts/boss_wheel_v2/` (coordinator, 2026-10-15). What is here is reference only.

## What exists (reference, not final)
- `boss_wheel_options.png`: three motifs on the unchanged round 41 D4 renderer: A DIVER (brass helmet hub, air hose
  wrapping the rim), B OCTOPUS (picked: sucker rim, four arms curling round the threat ring), C JELLYFISH (glass bell
  hub, photophore rim with a coral crust, tendrils trailing below).
- `boss_wheel.png`: option B finished.
- `scripts/wheel41.py`: the theme plug-in (see the Roman note); slice skin = deep gradient + caustic net + bubbles;
  scenes = harpoon, torpedo, bulkhead, air pocket, marine snow, and TIDE (the water level rising in the screen).

## Ideas worth keeping
1. **The octopus's ink is the black smoke.** The Central Server is THE BLACK SMOKER (a vent); an octopus boss lets the
   name, the vent and the emblem tell one story. Phase 2: the hub inks black (reuse the LOCKDOWN waterline, in ink).
2. **Arms break the circle.** It is the only option that changes the wheel's silhouette, so it reads at r = 60. Keep
   the arms off the blade, the readout rail (top) and the HP arc (bottom).
3. **TIDE on the wheel.** The house rule is a four-step tide (LOW, RISING, HIGH, FALLING). A tide gauge in the rail or
   a waterline across the slice screens at the current tide height would show it without a new HUD element.
4. Coral (the designer's list) works as a crust that grows on the rim with phases (P2 coral, P3 the arms).
5. Palette: abyss blue #2F6BFF (corp hue), lightless red #C8264E (tier II; it is close to HARM, so use it as material,
   not as a UI signal), trench #06112A, photophore #7CF2E4 world-only.
6. Emblem: `nc_kit.g_octopus` is the current pick; jellyfish (`g_jelly`) is a strong second.

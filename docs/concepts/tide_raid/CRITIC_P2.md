## Requested checks
- **Consistency.** Same game now, with one stop of grade drift and a few differing placements. The plate grade (saturated, deep violet with warm lamps) is richer; video and interact look duller and bluer on the left half. Decision for Godot: take the plate grade as the target. Interact uses the ring-less ground marks and stop marks differently (blue "legal target" ovals) that exist in no plate.
- **Forecast tile placement (02): a real bug.** In `02_forecast` the three tier tiles (HOLDS shield, intel tile with "1", UNTOUCHED target) sit in the bottom-right (about x1470-1810, y860-980), 1000+ px from the Sites in the upper-left cluster (x420-880, y400-600) and hang from long thin leader lines that cross the map. 04 repeats it (HOLDS shield at about 1550,940 with binary glyphs at the bottom edge) and 06 puts two orange rings at the bottom edge. The cause is `p1_final.tile()`: the anchor is `site['B'] - height*3.73 - up` plus a dx/dy that is set per beat, and the 02/04 beats call it with the wrong anchor (the `site['B']` of the plate-space vs ground-space, or the stop `sid` of a different route) so the tile ends in bottom-right. Either way, as drawn, the tile is not "on the Site" and the forecast beat, the key one, loses its in-place read (Into the Breach rule 3). Fix = anchor each tile 40-70 px above its glass tower (the tiles already exist correctly in interact 03a: shield above t2, heart at HOME).
- **Solace cage.** It is quiet enough against the beams (mint at 25-60 %, no beam hue), and framed top-right where the HOME is bottom-left, so they do not compete in position. But it is the biggest bright green object in every frame (a lit cylinder with a floating helix and 6 concentric rings), the only large saturated green on a blue/violet city, and in the interact frames it is at higher brightness than in the plates. At 320 grey it is the second-brightest cluster after HOME and the beam. Drop it another 25 % and take the lower rings to a thin line.
- **X-ray ground marks (interact frames at 55 % through buildings vs hidden).** Hidden reads cleaner: in 02b/03a the blue legal-target ovals and the yellow diamond sit as ghost marks through the towers and become part of the beam/tower tangle (see 03a, left cluster). The 55 % ghost does help when the Site is occluded (the stop block "found" behind a tower). Recommendation: hidden by default (as the contract says), and x-ray 55 % only for the one focused or hovered carrier and the committed preview, never for the ambient legal-target ovals.
- **Vision (living, vibrant night city).** Plates: yes, clearly better, a city with life beyond the route. Video/interact: dimmer and cooler.
- **No-panel.** A whole raid can run with no text panels: plates have none; interact uses pictogram rows and discs. The tally row and disc trios are still ground-level glyph clutter at the bottom left. Yes, but not yet "no clutter".
- **Squint 320.** Beam yes (01/03/04), HOME yes, defended Sites no (tier rings merge); tiles dot-level only. 02 is the worst: yellow, blue, magenta ribbons plus five towers become one web. 03 is the best (one lavender ribbon).
- **Colour-blind.** Deutan: pattern (chevron/chain/helix) carries; fine at 1920, weak at squint. Tritan: yellow E goes near-white and DAMAGED loses the amber; HOME-hit is carried by shape. Acceptable, not exemplary.
- **Clutter (t1 cluster).** Improved, still the densest spot: three glass towers, a ring, a stop block, up to 3 ribbons and the old-ring ghost within 220 px. In the interact frames add legal-target ovals and a tile and it tangles.
- **Godot buildability.** Good, same list as P1 plus: grade as a post shader (specified), tier decals, ranked towers, dread decal, 4K parity capture. Risks: depth-inverted ghost pass; the Bloom shell look (07) and the cracked-glass body need a custom shader; the wet-street reflection smear and sodium pools are comp-time (SSR or a planar pass); the 2D camera parallax in the videos will not match real geometry.

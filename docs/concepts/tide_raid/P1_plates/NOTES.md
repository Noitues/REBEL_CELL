# P1 golden plates, iteration 1 (Solace Infected Tide raid, "Jack-in over the Night City")

Seven 1920x1080 plates in `plates/` (01 plan, 02 forecast, 03 sealed/hunter reveal, 04 playout impact with shield, 05 HOME hit, 06 aftermath, 07 CITY LOST), `crops2x/` (hot spot, 960x540 window x2), `compare/` (pass-2 | P1), `squint/` (320 px, 4 px blur, `ALL_strip_320_and_blur4.png`), `colorblind/` (deutan, tritan, `ALL_normal_deutan_tritan.png`), `text_1to1/` (HUD and hot-spot at 1:1), `sheet.jpg`, `scripts/`.

## What changed against pass 2
Pass 2 was flat PIL overlays on a Godot capture. P1 is built on the real city geometry:
1. **The city is a depth-true holdout.** A new Godot capture (`scripts/p1_depth_capture.gd`, run from a scratch copy of the tide worktree, now removed) writes beauty, class id and a 16-bit depth pass at the raid camera (yaw 135, pitch 40) at the pass-2 framing (zoom 1.5 about plate px 722,500, ortho 394.59 BU wide). `recon.py` turns depth into world positions and `hfield.py` into a 0.6 BU height-field (H) plus a smooth terrain model (G). Blender (headless, GPU lock, never the designer's instance) gets H as a Holdout mesh: exact occlusion, and, with the plate as projected albedo, a mesh that real lights can hit.
2. **ICE towers are 3D volumes**: faceted glass (smooth-normal fresnel gradient, world-Z data scanlines, vertical fade), thin emissive wire beams (real geometry, they light facades), a spine and a ground pad. T1 prism with chamfer, T2 obelisk, T3 stepped spire; DOWN = upper volume torn away into slices. The Solace helix cage is a lattice cylinder.
3. **Beams are emissive ribbons** on the terrain (raised flat-top strip, ink casing, type pattern as texture: D chain links, N braid, E hazard chevrons), lanes +-21 px so D/N/E sit side by side. Dense skyline hides most street, so three passes are composited: occluded (bright), **ghost** (see-through, only where occluded, 0.8 gain, with a darkened corridor of the city under it), and **spill** (the city diffusely lit by ribbons, towers and flashes, plus a cold moon sun).
4. **Atmosphere in comp** (`p1_comp.py`, linear light): depth haze, rain streaks that brighten near lights, multi-scale bloom, vertical light columns, tilt-shift softening, filmic shoulder, vignette, edge fringe, grain.
5. **Grade** (art-pass review, "night but vivid"): saturation 1.45, mids held, indigo shadows, window yellow pulled to warm white so the only yellow is E; the off-route city dims only 28 %.
6. **Heads**: the designer-locked H6 family (D worm, N thorn claw, E sea-mine), 44 px, E 0.852x, as ONE isolated screen-space sprite layer (`p1_heads.py`, `sprite()` is the only swap point), drawn above beams and below Site tags/tiles/pips. N claw refined: thicker prongs, an asymmetric barb on the upper horn, shorter straight lower blade (reads as a claw, not a "3" or a pound sign, at the 8 headings checked).
7. **Palette rulings applied**: tier on the target only (bolder rings and stop blocks with hatch: HOLDS bars, SITES LOST diagonal, DAMAGED chevrons, BREACHED crosshatch); yellow = E; **DAMAGED FF6A1A -> FF9A00** (tritan dE vs N 23 -> 29, vs BREACHED 17 -> 42), and because that now sits close to amber, **SITES LOST F29A1A -> C98410** (flag for designer: a consequence of the ruling). HOME-hit non-hue cue: a jagged white-hot fracture star with ink-cut cracks plus six ticks thrown off the HOME ring (shape and luminance, not hue).

## What Godot needs to match each plate
Shared:
- **Camera**: raid view with `ortho = 394.59` (1.5x zoom of 591.89) and target shifted so plate px (722,500) is centred (`unproject` as in `p1_depth_capture.gd`).
- **Grade** (post shader or tide_look): saturate 1.45, `pow(c, 1.15)*0.92`, +indigo lift by (1-lum), hue band 8-76 deg (windows) desaturated 55 %, off-route multiplier 1 - 0.28*smoothstep(90,420 px from any route polyline). Palette: put DAMAGED FF9A00 and SITES_LOST C98410 in `Palette`.
- **Beam ribbon**: mesh strip on the terrain (lift 0.6 BU), width D 4.2 / N 4.4 / E 4.8 BU, UV u = arclength / tile (D 16, N 12, E 16 BU), emission texture `tex/ribbon_*.png` (generated by `p1_tex.py`), strength ~4.5, plus an **x-ray pass**: same mesh drawn depth-test-inverted at ~0.8 alpha where occluded, and a corridor darkening of the city under it (tide_fx / city3d grid network layer has the ground pass that Godot already shows under see-through buildings: reuse it).
- **ICE tower**: spatial shader, alpha, emission = fresnel(0.30) + scan lines every 1.7 BU (line 0.07) + vertical fade 0.35, wire as thin emissive box edges (r 0.085 BU) or a wireframe shader, spine r 0.28 BU, pad radial glow 3.4 a wide. Heights 27 / 35 / 44 BU (tiers 1-3), half-width 4.6 / 4.2 / 4.8. Casts light: a few emissive-plane GI probes or a screen-space bleed is enough.
- **Heads**: `H6_heads_final` sprites, billboard at the route position, heading = screen tangent, 44 px, above beam layer, below tags.
- **Spill / moon**: sun (cold 9DB4FF, energy ~1.6) and sky fill so facades have form; beams and flashes add coloured light (omni, short range).
- **Post**: bloom (sigmas 3/9/26/70), tall light-column glow, rain brightening near lights, tilt-shift 1.8 px at the top 16 % and bottom 14 %, vignette 0.34, edge fringe 1.6 px, grain 1.2 %.

Per beat:
- **01 plan / 02 forecast**: three ribbons parked heads; tier ring r 9.5-12.5 BU (flat emissive annulus, w 1.5, strength 7, plus a radial fill glow) at the target Site; stop block 11.5 x 9 BU quad across the street (`tex/block_<tier>.png`); guard wall = vertical 11x14 BU hex-lattice quad (`tex/wall_D.png`); HOME deck = hex prism + 50 ground ticks (lit warm white, preview 6 amber outlined, dark grey for gone). Tiles above the tower top, leaders hairline.
- **03 reveal**: sealed corridors = ground mist strips 10 BU wide (noise-gated emission, lavender 9C8CFF, no light emission); lock A becomes the type glyph; hunter streets are white dashed 1.4-2.2 BU ribbons; reticle and eye are 2D.
- **04 impact**: sentry dome (hemisphere r 11 BU, hex wire fresnel), burst = two radial glows (teal, white core), two shock rings, ~34 hex/tri/rect shards, a 30 kW omni light, particles ("bits") as 2D digits; held at the peak.
- **05 HOME hit**: same burst in DAMAGED (FF9A00), 6 ticks flying, glitch tear band, fracture-star sprite, HUD HOME chip flash, no shake in the plate (storyboard decides).
- **06 aftermath**: ribbons at 10 % (dead trails), DOWN grease marks, decay rings, wheatpaste poster (pass-2 `hy.poster` in FF9A00).
- **07 CITY LOST**: **grid Bloom API**: a field image over the city grid (R front value, hairline at 0.5, G climb height / 64 BU) drives an overlay shell on the city mesh: plum body 1E0A22 with Voronoi veins FF3FA8, hot hairline FFD6F0 at the front and up the facade climb edge, shell must not be sampled as a light (emission sampling off). `p1_bloom.make_field` builds the field with domain-warped distance from eaten Sites, HOME and street tendrils.

Scripts: `p1_beats.py <beat>` (spec), `run_bl.py spec log` (Blender under the GPU lock), `p1_final.py all` (compose + UI + finish), `p1_post.py` (strips, sims, crops, sheet). UI/type reuses the pass-2 kit (`H2_jack_in_settle/scripts`, re-coloured through its TIER table).

## Self-critique (honest)
Own score guess against the bar (S8 V9 Read9 Motion9 Wordless9): **S 8, V 8-9, Read 8, Wordless 8**. Not yet beating V or Readability, and Motion is not in this stage.
Strengths: real occlusion and light spill, a vivid lit city behind a lit route, a clear 3-body route at squint (320 px and blur strips read: HOME ring, route, tower cluster), impact flash and the CITY LOST climb read at a glance.
Weak points:
1. **Skyline hides the streets**, so most of every beam is ghost. The ghost keeps the route readable at 320 px but the "bright ribbon on a street" read is only where gaps allow; 03 (reveal) is the weakest here (fog corridors are pale lavender and thin).
2. **Hierarchy**: 01/02 still carry three overlapping beams plus 10 towers plus the cage; the cage and towers are brighter than "quiet slate" should be. Off-route towers need another 30 % dimming.
3. **Heads are small and mostly sprite-flat** (44 px, locked), not lit by the scene; the N claw is the weakest silhouette at the blur.
4. **06 aftermath** is nearly empty (poster plus two grease marks); 04's tile sits close to the flash.
5. **UI/type is pass-2's kit** (tiles, chips, cards, poster): clean but not re-modelled for final material quality; only the tile shadows and the fracture star are new.
6. **2x crops are upscaled, not re-rendered at 2x**; the Godot plate itself is 1920 native.
7. **CITY LOST** body reads magenta-red rather than deep plum, and the climb hairline is busy.
8. Deutan: D and N both go blue-lavender (rests on pattern and head shape); tritan DAMAGED is now a pale salmon, distinct from the N ribbon and BREACHED, but 05's burst still leans on shape/luminance, as designed.
9.

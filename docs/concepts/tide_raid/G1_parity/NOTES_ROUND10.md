## Night-light guide, cyberpunk refs, facade-light bug
- Lamps merge into lines (guide 1): DONE. `per_lot` = 2 lamps per kerb per street lot (one per 3 BU), sigma narrowed (core 0.45 BU, pool 0.75 BU), the field sums along a street (clamped at 1) so lamps read as a ribbon. At squint (final/01_plan_squint.png) the strings read as lines in the lower left; across the dense core the grid is still carried more by the facade base glow than by the street (gap below).
- Neon (guide 5-6, cyberpunk refs): PARTLY. `CityNeonKit` + `shaders/city/city_neon.gdshader`: 2 hotspots per territory (busy intersections ringed by buildings, 14+ lots apart; 8 in the plan), each with its own pair of neon colours (cyan, magenta, green, orange; cream accent). Inside a hotspot tall buildings carry giant screens and blade signs on the seen walls, some roofs a flat screen (soft scrolling bands and a little flicker), storefront glow strips at the foot of busy-street buildings (amber, a cool white second voice, neon-tinted in hotspots), a flat colour streak of each screen on the street in front of it, and cyan / white LED crowns on towers over 50 BU. The neon carries its own class tag (roughness 0.42) so the grade leaves its colour alone. Close-ups beside the Shibuya ref: refs_vs_r10_sheet.jpg, hotspot_r10_before_after.png, hotspot2_r10_before_after.png. NOT done: sign spill tinting nearby building bodies, bright white / amber crossings and lit-from-below dark trees inside hotspots, the wet streaks as long vertical reflections through the existing wet-reflection path (they are flat ground quads), the low haze glow over the densest district. Screens are plain rectangles, not Shibuya density.
- Highways glow from traffic (guide 2): DONE (dashes). Red tail-light and white headlight dash streams on every elevated highway deck and on the ramps (`traffic_*` config; brightness varies dash to dash by hash). No streetlights on highways or ramps (the exclusion zone follows the ramp polylines too). Visible in ix1 / ix2 close-ups.
- Facade lamp falloff bug: FIXED. Cause: the facade wash sampled the blurred street-pixel lamp field in screen space (a large radius, not tied to height, so it rose in wedges up the walls), and the ambient lamp bounce used the absolute world y, not the height above the local ground. Now the facade wash is the lamp field at the wall's world footprint (sigma `lamp_wall_sigma_bu` 1.8 BU: the reach from the kerb into the facade) times exp(-h / `lamp_wall_h0_bu`), h = height above the local terrain (3.0 BU: dark by about 3 floors), and the ambient bounce falls off with the height above the local ground (`amb_lamp_wall_reach_bu` 3.5). Upper floors take windows, crowns and signs only. Close-up: canyon_r10_before_after.png (the bright base fades upward into dark; before, the light came as wedges up the facades).

## 2. Highway interchanges: DONE (visual)
`CityInterchangeKit` (pure, A2 metres): finds every crossing of two elevated highway polylines (ring, motorway, truck road; 12 in the plan) and every road end that stops within 160 m of another (a T), and gives each up to four turn ramps (one per quadrant): a cubic Bezier tangent to the first deck `interchange_reach_bu` (30 BU) short of the crossing and tangent to the second deck that far past it, its height smoothly blending from one deck's lift to the other's, so a ramp joins deck to deck. Near-parallel roads (sine under 0.42), joins near tunnels and quadrants where a T road does not run get none. `CityA2Features` draws the ramp deck, edge, pillars, glow line and traffic (part "flyovers"). The two roads keep their own decks. The plan's old `interchanges` ramp stubs are unchanged. Close-ups: ix1_r10_before_after.png, ix2_r10_before_after.png. Tests: test_city_interchange_kit (5).
- Gap: ramps are Beziers between the decks; no diamond or cloverleaf loops.

## 4. Building shapes: DONE
`CityShapeKit`: per-building shape from a hash of the layout key (no RNG), weighted per territory and height (`shape_weights`): plain, setback / stepped tower, L, T, chamfered, rounded, cylinder, slender tower on a podium, slanted top; roof features: spire, antenna mast, rooftop units. Same vertex layout as the unit prisms so windows, ink and trim work; all shapes stay inside the unit square, so lot footprints and layout are unchanged (rotated by quarter turns in the instance transform). The sky-visibility height field uses the shape's own roof height; opaque occlusion is the building mesh itself. District character: Halcyon setbacks and rounds, Orbital cylinders and podium towers, Meridian blocks with L and chamfers, the Cell's L and T low-rise. Solace kits are left alone. Still MultiMesh instancing (more families per chunk). Close-up: shapes_r10_before_after.png. Tests: test_city_shape_kit (7).
- Gap: neon screens skip podium, L, T and cylinder walls.

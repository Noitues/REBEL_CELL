### Notes on the layout rules

- Street hierarchy: one global grid, arterials every 8-12 lots (continuous across districts), local streets splitting each cell with a period of 4-6 lots that drifts by at most one lot per cell. A ground lot with no street beside it merges with a neighbour that has one (a 1 x 2 building). no_frontage falls by about 85% against the P0 layout.
- Height field: block-scale value noise, the neighbouring territory's range blended across territory borders, a falloff from the Old City centre, a step-down beside parks and water, and low breaks steered into the 3-4% band of the dense core (CityLowRise is the one definition of a low break; the core excludes lots within 4 of water or a park, where low rise is by rule). height_cliff falls by about 90%.
- Waterfront and river: the first lot from the sea / a river is a promenade / embankment park, the lots within 2 (sea) and 3 (river) are low-rise. Dense-zone open land (under decks, hillsides, given-back streets) is park, never bare grey: bare_in_dense is 0. A one-lot park strip between two streets is built on (or, under a deck, becomes a service lane).
- Sprawl thins in whole 4 x 4 blocks. Ramp ends: a ground, bare or park lot under a ramp end becomes a flagged street lot.
- singleton_building excludes the Solace lawn blocks and a Site's own building (both stand apart by design); site_on_road_or_park is unchanged by P1 (Sites were not moved).

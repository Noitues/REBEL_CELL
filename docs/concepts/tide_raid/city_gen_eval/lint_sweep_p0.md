### Notes on the P0 layout rules

- P0 rules cleared: corridors (all four classes), street_touches_sea, street_in_park, street_components, dead_end, site_without_street are at 0 or inside budget on every seed.
- Declared exemptions (flags in `lots["flagged"]`, counted in `lots["street_info"]`): plaza (A2 stations), cul_de_sac (a Site's street end; dead-end runs of at most `city_a2_cul_de_sac_max` lots), quay (A2 roads on the water or along the shore, and local shore runs), plan_end (an A2 road ending on its own), site_street, solace_border (the Solace park outline needs its border streets). A Site and its 8 neighbours are exempt from the corridor rule.
- bare_in_dense rose ~2%: lots under a deck or a given-back street in the dense zone become park strips (`city_a2_fill_depth`), the rest of the rise is the Site ring staying built and the sprawl edge.
- no_frontage rose ~7% (cul-de-sacs of up to 8 lots keep most stub frontage): grid lines no longer cross parks and shore streets that ended in the sea are trimmed, so the buildings beside them lose a street side. P1 (street hierarchy, block merge) owns the rest.
- park_street_both_sides rose: the corridor park strips are park lots with a street on both sides. P1.
- site_on_road_or_park was re-specified: a Site on a LOCAL street lot is by design (Sites are laid out before the street grid; the grid runs through them and the building stands beside the Site). The rule now counts Sites on an A2 road, an A2 park (Solace lawn exempt) or water. The 'before' figure uses the old definition (any street lot), so the two are not comparable; 773 Sites over 25 seeds remain, all on A2 road or park lots where no clean Site heading exists.

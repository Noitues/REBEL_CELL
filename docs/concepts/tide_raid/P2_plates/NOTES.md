## Weak points
1. Tier rings are indistinguishable in greyscale at 320 (glyph tile carries it); stop blocks often sit in street gaps hidden by buildings, so mostly read via the ghost pass.
2. Plum creep reads slightly pink on the green park; impact core is still the brightest white in the frame (smaller, with streaks and a legible head).
3. Cards, chips and tiles are still the pass-2 kit (flat vs the city).
4. Spill pass is half-res 48 spp (low-frequency light), E bloom cut is by construction, 07 uses the default-scenario city lights (not dimmed).
5. The machine was CPU-saturated by other jobs for about 1.5 h (renders 40x slower); I changed nothing to cope except the spill pass cost.
6. Process slips: I typed a bare `python -` (empty heredoc) four times; each hung and was stopped with TaskStop, no work affected. A kill of my own stuck Blender PID was denied by the permission layer; I waited it out. No wildcard deletes, GPU lock taken, no background processes left. The scratch worktree `p2_wt` was removed with `git worktree remove`.

## What Godot needs (updated)
Everything in P1 NOTES "What Godot needs" still holds, plus:
- **Grade** = post shader: saturate 1.5, `pow(c,1.10)*0.95`, per-pixel warmth field (low-freq noise, south/left bias) driving body tint (cool 0.72,1.04,1.14 to warm 1.22,0.94,0.74) and light recolour (sodium 1.0,0.56,0.2 / cyan 0.25,0.85,1.0); lights = local contrast mask; water class teal; off-route dim 14 % lights exempt; `Palette`: DAMAGED FF9A00, SITES_LOST C98410 (ink plate + white rim + hatch).
- **Street layer**: sodium lamp pools on a world lattice (9 BU, 28 % on), SSR or a cheap planar wet pass on street class only (decal with vertical smear of emissive), signage = emissive quads on facades (15).
- **Rings / blocks**: decal quads with `tex/ring_<tier>.png` and `tex/block_<tier>.png` (ink under-stroke, white rim, pattern); HOME ring + 50 radial ticks + pool.
- **Towers**: bright set = stop Sites + DOWN; quiet set 35 % glass without wire / spine. Cage tint 55E0A0 at 25 %.
- **Pressure**: creep = world-space distance from the cage base, domain-warped, applied as an overlay (ground + facade climb by height above ground), hairline at the front; dread = a darkening decal following the route ahead of each head (300 px, 45-65 %); DOWN ring shader with an arc fraction uniform.
- **Impact**: cap flash max channel at 1.0 pre-tonemap, streaks as 18 thin radial quads, head sprite above with a 3 px ink halo.
- **Capture parity**: `scripts/p2_depth_capture` style capture at 3840x2160 with the pixel constants scaled (centre 1444,1000), 4K reproduces the plate exactly.
Scripts: `p1_beats.py` (specs), `run_beats.sh` (Blender under the GPU lock; `P2_S=2` for 4K), `p1_final.py all` (comp + UI, `P2_S=2` native), `down4k.py`, `p2_post.py`, `p2_base.py`.

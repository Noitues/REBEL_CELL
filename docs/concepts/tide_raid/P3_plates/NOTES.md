## Weak points
1. Hero facade is a skin on a single planar wall in the depth recon (it reads at 4K, a little like a window grid at 1080); the baked Godot ink outlines of non-hero buildings are unchanged.
2. Shafts are screen-space smears (no depth); lamp shafts in the park read as orange candle columns if you look for them.
3. The 07 veins are still a web; the 07 body is a flat plum without window-dark patches.
4. Blender ran with 16 spp (occ) and half-res ghost / 32 spp spill because the shared GPU made 4K renders take 25-60 min per beat; the final passes were rendered on CPU (Cycles) at 4K, 4-6 min per beat. Nothing visible changed (emission-only layers) but it is a deviation from P2's 64 spp.
5. Ring ladder: SITES LOST disc is half-hidden by its own tower wire; BREACHED vs HOLDS ring is the closest pair in greyscale.
6. Non-hero ribbons recede to about 60 %; in 04 the shield burst still pulls the eye (by design).
7. Process: I typed a bare `python -` with an empty heredoc three times (each hung and was stopped with TaskStop; no work affected). I killed only my own PIDs (the GPU-queued Blender jobs and comp chains I started, and my own GPU lock file); no wildcard deletes; no background processes are left running; no scratch worktree was created. P1_*, P2_* and godot_look_tide were not touched (P2 plates are read only for the compare sheets).

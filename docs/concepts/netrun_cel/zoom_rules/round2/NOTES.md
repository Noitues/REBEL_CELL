## 1. Framing (done as far as the real city allows)
`CityZoomFrame` (new, pure): `occupancy`, `score`, `best`, `plaza_lots`. A frame = frontage point (lot coordinates, the along-look coordinate on a lot boundary), an axis look direction and the eye `d = 39 BU` (6.5 lots) behind it. Score = 0.35 frontage row (mid-rise heights around 16.5 BU = 33 m, holes cost) + 0.20 open wedge between eye and facade + 0.20 the road width ACROSS the view (open run of lots along the row in front of the facade) + 0.25 backdrop (towers of at least 22 BU in 5 rows behind, about half the frame width). Gates: eye lot is a prism, wedge less than 0.9 clear, frontage fill under 0.6, a prism touching the frustum within 4 lots of the eye, slope eye vs frontage over 0.25 BU or under the row over 0.6 BU. All weights and targets are `zr_frame_*`.
- Scan of the whole city (165 x 165 lots, 4 looks): **no lot passes the default gates.** The real city has no plaza that is open for 6.5 lots in front of a mid-rise row (heights: p50 7.3 BU, p75 17.2, p90 28.6, max 40). With relaxed gates (`near_lots` 2, `min_open` 0.8, `min_fill` 0.4, `logs/frame_scan.log`) the best are: (28.5,1.0) S 0.608, (27.5,1.0) S 0.597, **(37.5,19.0) N 0.587** (row 0.33, fill 0.73, open 0.82, road across 12 lots, backdrop 0.62, tower share 0.73), (47,16.5) W 0.584.
- **The frame I shipped is lot (34.5,-9.0) looking N.** It scored 0.711 under an earlier, laxer version of the rule (row 0.44, fill 0.73, open 0.74, across 12 lots, backdrop 0.86, mean height 19.1 BU, tower share 0.55) and is gated out (score 0) by the final rules: open 0.74 is under 0.8. I picked it by eye among the renders because (37.5,19.0) N rendered as a crowded canyon with a pink slab and a highway pylon (it is in `final/` scratch, not shipped). So **the framing choice is by eye, not by the score**, and the score of the shipped frame is "gated, 0". The score code and the scan are the deliverable; a plaza does not exist in this city seed.
- The occluder system (hide/flatten) already clears the lots right in front of the lens; the new `plaza_lots` makes the wedge lots that no visible prism covers wet forecourt (config `zr_plaza_on`). In the shipped frame the foreground is dominated by the reflection of the frontage and the park trees, not by a road (see gap list).
- Tower fade rule (config `zr_tfade_*`, shader `city_building.gdshader`, CPU mirror `CityZoomRules.tower_fade`): the shaft above 35 m (17.5 BU) blends toward the sky value with height (full at 80 m), and whole buildings taller than 35 m blend with distance from 30 to 150 BU; share 0.8 at most, lit-window emission x0.35 at full fade, the top 3 BU (crown) keeps its light. Bodies under 35 m are never touched (tested at every distance). Visible in the frame as the lavender, lower-contrast towers behind the row.
- Facade body above 3-8 BU is dimmed (`zr_body_dim` 0.8 base, 0.7 lit emission) so the band can be the bright horizontal.

## 3. The dominant pink/white panel (found and fixed)
It was a neon-kit wall **"screen"** quad (cat "screen", the round-12 block-pattern shader), not a holo, kit or facade. Round 1's `filter_neon` kept the one screen whose projected area was under the cap, but it measured the area **clamped to the viewport**: a tall wall screen cut off by the top of the frame measured 5.7 % and passed as "the hero". Fix: `CityZoomStreet.screen_area(..., clip=false)` (whole projected area) plus a T1 size limit (`CityZoomHero.size_ok`: at most `zr_hero_max_bu` 8 BU long, 4 BU short, i.e. 16 x 8 m), the pick itself is `CityZoomHero.pick` (one hero, under `zr_hero_cap` = 6 %, ties by id). After the fix 633 of the 1262 screens are rejected by size (629 stay candidates), the cap then keeps one; the slab is gone in its round-1 frame (verified at lot (-32,-8) W). I also added `zr_kit_k` (Solace kit emission at the street camera, 0.3) while hunting the slab; it was not the cause and is harmless, kept only because it is a view uniform with a test-free default of 1.0 off the street camera (remove if unwanted).

## 4. Brightness, road, cars, lamps, mist (partly)
Done in code: storefront window gain x `zr_band_gain` 1.6; lamp pool radius 4 BU at gain 0.9 (`zr_pool_*`), lamp head minimum 4.5 px; **car light streaks** (`CityZoomRoad.streaks_for`): 64 cars (128 quads), nearest lots first, one corp per car by the traffic branch's draw (`CityCorpColor.weights`, salt 4409 + seed % 997), head = lerp(corp, white, 0.6), tail = corp hue at V 0.55, two quads per car (head half, tail half), at least 2.5 px thick at any ppb, cars also run on the plaza lots within 2 lots of the frontage line; **ground mist** (`CityZoomSky.mist_card`: two additive gradient cards at 0.55 and 0.94 of the eye distance, 3 BU high, alpha 0.16).
**Not achieved in the frame:** the bottom 20 % shows the frontage's own reflection (the mirror pass) and park trees, not a wet road with streaks. The streaks and the lamp row exist in the data (`streak_count` 128) but are not visible in the shipped frame because no road lots are in the lower part of the view of that lot. Luminance profile (`scripts/lum_profile.py`): plate brightest 24 px row at 73 % down, ratio to the facade mean (25-60 % of height) **2.72**; ours **1.58** (round 1: 1.31); frame mean 0.24 vs plate 0.20 (round 1 0.28). The band is brighter than the facade now, but not by the 2.5x target, and the brightest horizontal is still at 91 % down (the reflection), not on the storefront line. Horizon stayed 0.74; I did not try the 0.68 fallback (the road is missing for a lot-content reason, not a framing one).

## 6. Deterministic capture (done: bit-exact)
`CityView3D.set_zoom_freeze(true)` (also `CityConfig.zr_freeze`): the post and water clocks stop, `CityMotionClock.frozen` zeroes every motion layer (street cars, sky cars, aviation, billboards...) every frame, the global shader switch `reduce_effects` goes to 1 so every `rc_time(TIME)` (neon screens, holo scanlines) reads 0, the ambient beams stop swaying. The first attempt left the layer clocks at whatever time they had reached while the city loaded (differs per process); `advance` now resets them to 0 when frozen.
**City-band diff (stage 1, ppb 4.9, three fresh processes OFF, ON, OFF): 0 differing pixels of 2 073 600 for ON vs OFF1, ON vs OFF2 and OFF1 vs OFF2.** (Round 1: 374 px outside the noise floor, 57 k noise.) Logs in `logs/ident_*.log`.

## Perf (GPU time of the city viewport, 500 frames, run_windowed, 1920x1080, frozen capture, the GPU shared with another agent)
First measurement after the round-2 additions: street p95 **13.8-15.7 ms** (limit 12.57): the mirror camera drew the whole city (far plane 4000 BU). Fixes: mirror far plane = 1.9 x the eye distance (`zr_refl_far_k`), and the mirror re-renders only every 30th frame once the camera has been still for 3 frames (`reflect_mode`, `zr_refl_idle_frames`; every frame while the camera moves). After: **Street band p95 7.62 / 8.07 / 8.53 ms** in three runs (mean 5.05-5.32, p99 7.9-9.4; `logs/perf_street_run*.log`), District band p95 7.1 / 9.2 / 7.8 ms. Limit 12.57 ms. Caveat: with the other agent's jobs on the same GPU the run-to-run clock state varies (the same build measured p50 4.3 to 7.1 ms), so read the three runs as a band, not a point; the frame time is dominated by the mirror pass, which is now off most frames. While the camera moves (the dolly) every frame pays the mirror again, at the far plane 1.9 x: not measured separately.

## Self-review against the plate (`ref_vs_street.jpg`)
**Matched:** a row of mid-rises (about 10-19 BU, 20-38 m) across the frame with taller towers behind them; the towers behind are lower-contrast, lavender silhouettes with sparse windows and a few lit crowns (the plate's layered depth); sign text at 0.4-0.6 m, readable fascia names; blade, wall-panel and neon-framed boards under the tier caps; the dominant slab is gone; sky gradient and violet fog as before; the City band is bit-exact.
**Not matched:** (1) no wide wet street: the lower 20 % is the frontage's reflection plus trees, so there are no car streaks, no road reflections of signs, no bokeh and no visible lamp row with pools; (2) the brightest horizontal is not the storefront line (91 % down vs 73 %), ratio 1.58 vs 2.72; (3) the facade is flat colour with few rectangles (no AC boxes, balconies, pipes, tanks); (4) no searchlights in this frame (the beams exist, none falls in view), no hero billboard "like the plate"; (5) a highway pylon cuts the right third; (6) palette is blue-violet, the plate is magenta on near-black; (7) the frame was chosen by eye because no lot passes the framing gates.
**Squint (squint_street.png vs squint_plate_street.png): FAIL, weaker than round 1.** At 1/16 area the ours reads as a field of lavender towers with a thin lit band of signs at the bottom, a clear mid-rise / tower layering, and no slab; the plate reads as dark towers over one bright magenta/orange horizontal with a wet foreground. Our bottom edge is the dim reflection, so the single bright horizontal the plate hangs on is missing.

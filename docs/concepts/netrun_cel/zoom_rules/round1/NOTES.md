## Item by item
1. **Camera and framing: done.** `CityZoomRules.street_cam` (fov 40 vertical, eye 2 BU over the ground, horizon at 0.74 by a frustum lens shift, near 0.25, distance from ppb target 38). `CityIsoCamera` gained `shift_y` and `near_bu` (0 = as before; ignored by orthographic cameras). `CityView3D.set_iso` switches the camera to `PROJECTION_FRUSTUM` (size = frame height at the near plane) only when the rules are on. Pixel-constant ink: `CityZoomRules.ink_width` per band (2.0 / 1.6 / 1.4 / 1.2 px), blended in over the City -> District zone from the r43 formula, which stays exactly as is below ppb 6.4. Dolly zoom: `CityZoomRules.dolly_cam` (log-space ppb, fov 8 -> 40, pitch, yaw, shift). Missing: the ortho -> perspective swap is a plain cut at the same ppb (fov 8 start), not yet cross-checked frame by frame; the band tracker with dwell (`CityZoomStreet.band`) exists but only the hysteresis function is wired to nothing visible yet.
2. **Occluders and lens clutter: core done.** `CityZoomOcclude.classify` (corridor triangle with 3 BU padding, 12 BU hide distance, >25% hides, else flattens near-side buildings in the frame). Hide = the instance dissolves (Bayer discard through the existing `NetrunInstancePack` bits; the building shader decodes them when `zr_on`), flatten = cut to the 4 BU podium. At full melt the chunks are rebuilt without the building, its roof props and its neon (`zr_set_skip`; one hitch per rebuild, acceptable offline, not smooth in play). Trees inside the forecourt disc are discarded in `city_ground.gdshader` (`zr_clear`). Lens rule (`lens_suppress`, `in_push_zone`) drives the street lamps. Missing: host classes (no node anchors in round 1, `hosts` is empty), push-aside moves nothing (a lamp in the central 40% is dropped, not shifted), wires/sky cars/street cars are not touched, flattened buildings keep no roof props only because the props are dropped (not re-seated on the podium), hard-coded to one camera pose per rebuild key.
3. **Sky, fog, haze, searchlights: partly done.** Sky gradient (3 stops) and the fog curve `1 - exp(-(d/D)^1.4)` (D 130 BU, clamp 0.9, tint toward sky with height) are in `city_post.gdshader`, uniform-gated (`zr_on`, `zr_k`), the old ortho-scaled haze is switched off while they run. Haze cards (30/90/225 BU, additive) and the ambient beams (`CityZoomSky`: 2 beams from the tallest towers, tilt/azimuth/period hashed from the key, sway unless reduce-motion) are built. Missing: the beams are not visible in the street frame (the canyon leaves almost no sky) and I did not verify them in a wider frame; the 3 depth layers are not measured against the 0.18/0.26/0.34/0.42 value targets; the sky has no per-corp tint.
4. **Facade grammar: done in the shader** (`zr_facade.gdshaderinc`, called from `city_building.gdshader`). City cell 3.0 x 3.6 splits 2 x 2 into 1.5 x 1.8 (parent lit state keeps one lit child, so a window stays where it was); panel kinds (dark glass 56, warm/teal/white lit, AC box with vent line, louvre, blinds, wall) by hash; lit share by zone (0.60 / 0.38 / 0.24 / 0.12) with a per-building bias, column clustering, whole-floor ribbons above storey 15 on towers; wall tone steps by height and by 8% at each mechanical band; floor lines; louvred mechanical band with a thin tube every 11-15 BU in the shaft; a vertical corner neon on 1 building in 3. Missing: the plan-level "no blank wall" assert (the shader guarantees floor lines and kinds, but I did not write the plan-side check), pilasters, balcony/AC/pipe/fire-escape geometry (item 9), crown rings, side/back wall rule (all walls get the same grammar).
5. **Storefront band: done at real scale.** `CityStreetPlan.wall`: every street-facing wall gets a continuous band (plum backing, pilasters, bays 1.8-2.6 BU, a lit shop window with goods on two shelves and a mullion, a door recess on 1 bay in 3, two-tone awnings, OPEN/MENU boards on 1 in 4, hanging lanterns on 1 in 5), a fascia sign per bay with text (neon tube frame on 1 building in 3, lit box face with dark text on the rest), pavement slab, gutter, kerb, and a shop spill on the pavement. A BLANK wall draws as a shutter, never a hole. Missing: the fascia text is cut at wall ends (letters overlap on short walls), there are no pilaster-aware corner returns, the band mean luminance is not measured against the 2.5x target, kerb/pavement stop at wall ends.
6. **Wet road: done as a core version.** `city_zr_road.gdshader` over each street lot near the camera (`CityZoomRoad`): asphalt noise, patches, tyre tracks, utility plates, edge lines, dashed centre / double yellow, mid-block zebra, a wetness map (puddles + gutters, world space), a Fresnel term and a planar reflection (second camera mirrored about the street plane, 1/4 resolution SubViewport, ripple warp, 5-tap vertical smear). The lots of hidden buildings become wet "forecourt" asphalt (no markings), which is what gives the plate its big wet foreground. 24 additive car streaks per rebuild (colours weighted per the spec). SSR was not tried. Missing: manholes, steam, crack lines, tactile strips, kerb drains, bike lanes, parking bays; the forecourt only covers lots of hidden prisms (empty lots and the lawn stay as they are); in the street frame the wet road is a small part of the picture because the frontage is a canyon, not a plaza (see the gap list); streak drift is static.
7. **Sign hierarchy: partly done.** Fascia (T5) with a 3 x 5 glyph font from the invented name list, neon share 0.34 (tested within 0.04), lit-box faces at `zr_gain_box`, two hues per building max (`bay_hue`). `CityZoomStreet.filter_neon` caps the existing neon-kit screens to one hero under `zr_hero_cap` (4% of the frame): 1262 screens seen, 1261 dropped in the street frame. Missing: T2/T3/T4 are not rebuilt under their caps (the prototype's blade/roof signs are not on in zr mode), no SDF atlas (glyph runs as quads), and one very large pink/white panel still dominates the left tower in the street frame: it is not one of the neon-kit screen quads (the filter reports one kept and that one is small), so its source is unidentified. This is the main sign-hierarchy failure of the round.
8. **Lamps, clutter, min-pixel rule: partly done.** Real-scale lamps (`CityStreetPlan.lamps`: pole 7 m, head 0.8 x 0.3 m, arm, glow pool, 1 in 12 out) sized with `max(real, min_px / ppb)` (`CityZoomRules.min_px_size`), spaced every 9 BU along the kerb, dropped when `lens_suppress` or the central push zone says so, capped at 60. The hanging lanterns of item 5 are the only other clutter. Missing: bollards, vending machines, bins, crates, steam vents, wires, street signs, stalls; the r43 toy lamps are not replaced in the City/District bands (the new lamps appear from ppb 16); lamps are hard to see in the street frame.

## City band identity
`zoom_rules_on` off: no node is created, every uniform is 0, the camera code path is unchanged (`near` 1.0, no frustum). With it on, the City band is untouched by construction (ink formula blended in from ppb 6.4, `zr_on` post gate from 12.8, building grammar from 20, nothing built below 16). Image proof, stage 1 (ppb 4.9), `tools` freeze mode, three fresh processes (OFF, ON, OFF): the engine is not bit-deterministic (traffic, lights and rain move: two OFF renders differ on 57k pixels by more than 2 levels), so the diff is read against that floor. Outside a 5 px dilation of the pixels that move between the two OFF renders, ON vs OFF differs by more than 2 levels on **374 of 1.88 M pixels (0.02%)** and by exactly 1 level (dither) on ~40k; inside the animated area ON vs OFF differs like OFF vs OFF does. So the City band is r43 up to the engine's own noise, but I could not make it a bit-exact diff; a deterministic capture mode (frozen traffic and lights) would close that. Note `building_lod` is hysteretic: a capture after stage 3 starts at LOD 0, so each side of the comparison ran in its own process at stage 1.

## Gap to the plate (`ref_vs_street.jpg`)
Matched now: a frontal, long-lens frame (parallel verticals, horizon at 0.74, no near-plane cut, pixel-constant ink); a plum sky that fades down to a lighter horizon band and fog that grades the far towers; a street-scale facade (1.5 x 1.8 module windows with frames and mullions, panel kinds, sparse lit share, tone steps, floor lines, louvred mechanical bands, a corner neon on 1 tower in 3); a continuous storefront band at the foot of every frontage with lit shop windows, awnings and a text fascia sign on every bay; wet forecourt asphalt with markings and a mirrored reflection where the camera has road in view; the toy trees are gone from the lens zone.
Not matched: (1) **no wide frontage row**: the plate is a ~100 m row of mid-rises (33 m) with many small rectangles, ours is a canyon of 60-80 m towers, so the storefront band is a thin bar at the bottom edge and the wet road is a sliver; the frame shows what the city has, the camera cannot make it a plaza without choosing a frontage with low buildings; (2) **one pink/white panel dominates** the left tower (the sign cap is not enforced on it); (3) the plate's brightest horizontal is a long neon + reflection band, ours is dim by comparison and the road reflection is faint; (4) sign hierarchy: no roof signs, no blade signs, no hero billboard shaped like the plate's; text is blocky 3 x 5 and clipped at wall ends; (5) no ambient searchlights visible, no car-light streaks that read (24 streaks, thin, mostly out of frame), no bokeh, no lamp row with glow pools (lamps exist, tiny and few in frame), no ground mist; (6) the towers are flat colour with few small rectangles, not the plate's many pale AC boxes, balconies, pipes, water tanks; (7) the palette is blue-violet, the plate is magenta/pink on near-black.
**Squint verdict for the street frame: fail (weak).** At 1/16 area the frame reads as dark towers with one bright white/pink slab and a thin bright bar at the bottom edge; the bright storefront horizontal the plate has does not carry the frame, and the slab breaks the "no dominant sign" rule. Near-street and district squints hold up (the district keeps the park/tower read; the near-street dolly frame shows the forecourt, markings and a continuous band).

## What the next round needs first
1. Find and cap the dominant pink/white panel (probably the per-building `sign_share`/art-district strip in the building shader or a landmark kit, not a neon-kit quad).
2. Frame selection: pick frontages with 20-40 m buildings (or low the horizon, spec 5.1 fallback 0.68) so the band and the road carry the frame; this is a content/placement question, route logic of item 10 would own it.
3. Raise the storefront band and road value (spec: band >= 2.5x the facade mean); more streaks and the lamp row with pools; sign tiers T2-T4 under caps.
4. Make the capture deterministic (freeze the motion clock) so the City-band diff can be bit-exact.
5. Trees (item 9) and the facade modules, which round 1 did not touch.

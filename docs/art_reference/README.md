# Art reference (curated)

The locked reference images from the concept direction (rounds 1–43) that ART_BIBLE v2
(`docs/ART_BIBLE.md`) Appendix A and its §3.20 FX table name, curated for main (designer ruling 3,
DECISIONS "2026-10-05 — Designer rulings: art reintegration, pause point 0"). The full concept archive
(≈277 MB: every round, rejected and superseded variants, the Blender/PIL scripts, the evolution
slideshow and video) stays on tag `art-concepts-r43` (`9a62cec`). Landed by ART-0a; ported from
art-pass 9a62cec.

- **Layout:** `<area>/<original round folder>/<file>`; areas: `foundations`, `wheel`, `cards_fx`, `hud`, `city`, `raid`, `netrun`, `hq`, `shop_events`, `portraits`, `menus`, `campaign_end`, `glyphs`, `fonts`. Glyph masters are flat in `glyphs/`, the Courier Prime faces and their OFL in `fonts/`.
- **Copies:** stills up to 600 KB and 1600 px wide, every image with alpha (the glyphs), and GIFs up
  to 1.0 MB are byte-identical originals. Larger stills are downscaled copies, 1600 px wide JPEG
  q82 (wide strips, aspect ≥ 2.5, keep their width), so the name ends in `.jpg`. No Appendix A
  image is copied at full resolution when it is over 2 MB: the 80 MB cap and every ART-n worktree
  being a full checkout win; the original is one `git show` away (below). Larger GIFs are replaced by
  a keyframe sheet `<name>.frames.jpg` (8 frames sampled evenly in time, each labelled with its
  time) or, where the art pass made one, by its own strip or storyboard; the GIF itself is listed
  in "Not copied, why".
- **Original at full size:** `git show art-concepts-r43:docs/concepts/<original path> > %TEMP%\x.<ext>`
  (read only; never merge the tag).
- Godot never imports this folder (`.gdignore`). Total: 60.6 MB, 258 files.
